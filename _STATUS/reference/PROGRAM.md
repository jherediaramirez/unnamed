## 5. Landing protocol (one merge at a time, handbook 4.7 A5)

1. A step is committed and reviewed in its lane (worker template: clean Sonnet 5.5 review, suites green, SM approval).
2. The SM asks the PM: `TOKEN? <track> <step> <lane HEAD>`. The PM grants one token at a time, critical path first (T1 > T2 > T3 > T4 > T5 > T6 when requests tie; exception, PM 2026-10-14: T4's step 15 goes right after T1, since its landing frees T5's slot).
3. With the token, the SM gives the worker the merge go: "commit when green → merge `feature/survival` once (never rebase or force) → rebuild with touched sources or `-DisableAdaptiveUnity` → `Scripts\RunChecks.ps1 -Profile merge` → one combined report; stop on red."
4. The SM verifies: `pwsh -NoProfile -File C:\dev\tools\shift-managers\scripts\verify_lane.ps1 -Worktree <lane> -Base <feature/survival before the merge> -Summary <summary.txt>`, then sends the PM `LAND <track> <step> <lane HEAD> <summary.txt> <one-line what changed>`.
5. The PM runs `survival\scripts\verify_survival.ps1 -FastForward <lane HEAD> -WhatIf`, then without `-WhatIf`, pushes `feature/survival`, logs it in `LANDINGS.md`, sends SM-Codex `REVIEW <n> <from>..<to> <track> <step> <SM>`, returns the token and broadcasts `landed <hash> <track> <step>` to every SM. SMs pass it on to their running worker as "merge `feature/survival` at your next merge point" (not mid-step).
6. A lane whose landing waits keeps building its next step; it never waits idle for the token.
7. SM-Codex's `FIX? <report> <count>` (Codex findings it verified) goes into the owning track's next step, never mid-step; the SM logs each outcome in its track file. SMs may send SM-Codex `CODEX? <question> <files>` for an independent cross-check.

**Generated binary assets (asset locks):** `DA_Items` (+ `/Game/Items` sprites), `DA_Blocks` (+ `/Game/Blocks`, block materials) and `DA_Sounds` can't be merged by git. Only the lane holding that asset's lock may change it; the PM grants each lock (logged in `LANDINGS.md`) and it returns when that change lands. `DA_Blocks` and `DA_Sounds` stay with T5 for the program (no other track changes them). `DA_Items` is shared: ask "LOCK? DA_Items <track> <step>" before the step's plan is approved. A lane merging `feature/survival` never hand-merges one of these: it takes the incoming asset and re-runs its own import on top, then shows the import's verify output in the merge report. New assets (a new `DA_*` of a lane's own) need no lock.

**World hashes and goldens:** the program changes no world output. A step that would move a world hash or a golden stops and reports to its SM, who reports to the PM (only the PM can decide a survival hashes file). RunChecks uses the default `expected-hashes-main.txt` up to 540cfd23; **from landing 36 (ad045c40) every survival lane passes `-ExpectedHashes C:dev	oolsshift-managerssurvivalexpected-hashes-survival.txt`** (PM 5: r8 amplified moved by Jesus's firefly_bush → short_grass tap; main's file stays for feature/voxel-world until the final merge takes the survival values).
