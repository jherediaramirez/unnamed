- **The open state** (`UpdateBarrelOpen`, R16 `survival2.r16.animation_and_viewers`): viewers are players with a Barrel menu at the cell. Called after a committed open (and for a barrel the new menu replaces), a committed close (including `StepOpenMenus`' invalid-menu close) and `RemovePlayer`. On a change it writes `open` (plain `World.Set`, no place event) and adds a `BlockUsed` event: the sound, `Amount` 0.5, `LocationCm` the facing face's centre. No sound when the block is no longer a barrel (broken while viewed).
- **The sound place** differs from the handoff: R16's barrel rows play at z …775.0 where the chest's is at …775.5, facing north, so the barrel's sound is at the centre plus half a block along its facing. SM-Containers agreed.
- **B3a's `BlockUsed`** is reused, so no new event kind and no event-name table change. `CommitBlockUse` is not called: it would overwrite the open's `Result` (`OpenedContainer`), runs `SettlePlants`, and the close paths have no use result. The view plays `LocationCm` / `Amount` when `Amount > 0`, else B3a's cell centre at volume 1, so the doors are unchanged.
- **Entity cleanup:** `StepFurnaces` now drops any entity whose block is gone (it skipped non-furnaces before); only furnaces tick.
- **The screen:** `FHudContainerModel::GetHeight(Kind)` (168 for the barrel) for the frame origin and the hit test; `HasRecipeButton` (none for the barrel); the barrel's positions (8,18 / 8,85 / 8,143). `SHudContainerScreen` draws `generic_54` in two parts as ContainerScreen does, "Barrel" at 8,6 and "Inventory" at 8,74, no silhouettes.
