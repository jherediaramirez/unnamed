## 1. What changed

- **Hoe and shovel use** (`FFoundationAuthority::TillOn`, one call line in `UseOn` after `StripOn`; tables in the new
  `FoundationTilling.h/.cpp`, tags `Item.Hoes` and `Item.Shovels` from the tag ini). Hoe: dirt, grass block and dirt
  path to farmland, coarse dirt to dirt, rooted dirt to dirt plus one hanging roots item entity out of the clicked face.
  Shovel: grass block, dirt, coarse dirt, mycelium, podzol and rooted dirt to a dirt path. Top or side face and air
  above (not for rooted dirt's hoe row); 1 wear (none in creative or with wear off); adventure passes; the till settles
  the neighbours (a wall torch beside a new path pops: `block_support.json` gives farmland and the path no torch face).
  Events `Tilled` and `Flattened` (appended last; the controller's names and static_assert), sounds `item.hoe.till` and
  `item.shovel.flatten` at volume 1, pitch 1 (SURVIVAL-FINDINGS 3633/3635; already frozen events, already in
  `DA_Sounds`).
  Sources: minecraft.wiki "Hoe", "Shovel", "Dirt Path", "Rooted Dirt", fetched 2026-10-15 (the fetched pages show no
  revision date). The hoe's face rule and rooted dirt's any-face rule are provisional rows.
- **Turning to dirt** (`FoundationBlockFamilies`): farmland and the dirt path survive only under the 133 blocks of
  `family_survival.json`'s "up" set (signature 19; the other probes are signature 18, every candidate). Done by block
  id, not as a new `EFoundationBlockFamily`, so the shape code is untouched. `SettlePlants`' `Settle` writes dirt
  (`GetTurnedState`) instead of popping: no drop, no `Popped`. Placing either under a block it does not survive places
  dirt (`GetStateForPlacement`; behind `foundation.Blocks.Shapes` like every family placement).
- **Switch** `foundation.Blocks.Farmland` (default 1; Breaking.md).
- **Docs:** Blocks.md "Farmland, dirt paths, the hoe and the shovel (B5)" and the tests; Breaking.md; provisional rows
  (hoe face, rooted dirt, trampling's design note).
