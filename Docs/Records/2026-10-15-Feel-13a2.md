## What changed

- **Why the footsteps were hard to hear:** the player had no footstep code at all. Only mobs stepped.
- **Player footsteps** (`foundation.Player.Footsteps`, default 1; 0 is the old behaviour, with no player steps):
  - `Foundation/FoundationFootsteps.{h,cpp}` holds the pure cadence and the block lookup.
  - `AFoundationPawn` advances the cadence once per reference step and counts the steps.
  - `UFoundationInteractionView::PlayFootsteps` plays each new count through `FFoundationBreakEffects::PlayFootstep`. That call uses its own random stream, so no other sound's file draws shift.
  - **Volume and pitch:** the block's `DA_Sounds` step event at the sound type's volume × 0.15 and pitch × 1 [survival.r5.play_calls, F 3563].
  - **Cadence (provisional, wiki; R24 requested):** the distance grows by the horizontal move × 0.6 blocks. A step plays when the distance passes the next whole number.
  - **Silent cases:** sneaking and water pass due steps silently, with no backlog when they end. Flying and spectators don't step. A step that falls due in the air plays on landing.
  - **The block stepped on:** the block 0.2 blocks under the feet. When that cell is air, a fence, wall or fence gate below it counts.
  - **Combination blocks:** a moss carpet or snow layer (`#combination_step_sound_blocks`) plays first, then its support at 1/3 of the volume and pitch × 0.8 (provisional). Wool carpets aren't in the block set yet.
- **Before and after:** 0 player steps before. After: about 2.59 steps a second walking and 3.37 sprinting, at volume 0.15 (stone: `block.stone.step`, 0.15 and 1.0; metal blocks at pitch 1.5).
- **Master Volume (`sound_master`)** is now applied in game, as the audio device's primary volume.
  - Owner: `Menu/MenuAudioSettings.{h,cpp}` (`UMenuAudioSettings`, a GameInstance subsystem and tickable). It re-reads on every `UMenuPreferences` revision and on an audio device change.
  - The other category sliders are still saved only.
- **Bass (`sound_bass`, new preference 102; beyond vanilla by Jesus's choice):**
  - **What it is:** a low shelf on the master submix (`Menu/MenuBassShelfPreset.{h,cpp}`, `UMenuBassShelfPreset` / `FMenuBassShelf`), applied after every sound.
  - **Values:** corner 175 Hz, the engine's RBJ shelf with slope 1, gain = value × 5 dB, and a makeup of −0.3 × gain (−1.5 dB at +5).
  - **Default:** 1 (+5 dB) for every profile, including saved profiles that don't have the key.
  - **Off:** 0 removes the effect, which gives exactly vanilla's output. `menu.Audio.Bass 0` never attaches it.
  - **Device handling:** the effect is tracked by device id, removed before every add (never two shelves), and detached through the device manager on shutdown.
  - **Menu:** a "Bass" slider after UI in Music & Sounds (`MenuWeb/Patches/Preferences.mjs`, regenerated by `node MenuWeb/AdaptMenu.mjs`). The count is now 102 (`MenuPreferenceSchema.mjs`, its tests, `Tests/preferences-*.mjs`). It's stored in `Saved/Config/MenuPreferences.json`, with no ID renamed.
- **Unchanged:** no DA_Sounds or other asset change, no goldens or world hashes, and no hot file except `Docs/Records/README.md`.
- **Build:** `Unnamed.Build.cs` adds `AudioMixer` and `SignalProcessing`.
- **Docs:** `Breaking.md` (player footsteps), `MenuPreferences.md`, `Architecture.md`, `FunctionalBacklog.md`, `KnownDifferences.md`. Provisional rows (T4 13a2) are in `C:\dev\tools\shift-managers\survival\provisional-values.md`.

## Proof without recording (offline render)

`Unnamed.Menu.Audio.BassShelf` renders 1 s sines through the shelf at 48 kHz. The RMS gain out over in, after 0.25 s:

| Frequency | Measured (dB) | Shelf alone (dB) |
|---|---|---|
| 50 Hz | +3.463 | +4.965 |
| 100 Hz | +2.992 | +4.499 |
| 200 Hz | +0.367 | +1.864 |
| 1 kHz | −1.495 | +0.005 |
| 5 kHz | −1.500 | +0.000 |

- The measured column includes the −1.5 dB makeup. Without it, the boost is about +5 dB at 50 Hz, +1.9 dB at 200 Hz and 0 at 1 kHz.
- At 0 dB the shelf passes 50 Hz and 1 kHz unchanged.
- `BassEffect` runs the real submix effect on stereo buffers: 50 Hz left +3.463 dB and 1 kHz right −1.495 dB, so each channel keeps its own filter state.
