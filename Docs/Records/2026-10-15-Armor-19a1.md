## 1. What changed

- **Push-out (Codex review 11 #1, FX-a), replaced by vanilla's measured rule.** SM-Player added SURVIVAL-RUN2-FINDINGS during the step; its R23 (`survival2.r23.item_rules`, traced by `item_pushout`) and R19 replace the items fixes' provisional push-out and the first 19a1 fix for it. A body overlapping a block heads out of the cell holding its centre, by the nearest of north (-Y), south, west (-X), east and up (in that order; strictly nearer wins; never down); a neighbour one box fills whole is no way out (so a slab is one: FX-a); up by default. The axis gets 0.1 to 0.3 blocks per tick (a draw from the entity's id and age), the other axes keep 0.75, gravity (not for orbs) and drag still apply, and it moves through blocks. There is no "centre clear" branch any more, which is where the ping-pong lived. `MoveBox` gained the draw and `bGravityInside`.
- **Codex review 11 #2-#5:** a dead player's throwing click and close are refused (`Dead`); the sound log records whether the wave was loaded and probe 13d checks it; `Docs/Items.md`'s merge line; the push-out at switch 0 is documented as part of the motion existing drops and orbs keep (help texts and docs).
- **Codex review 14:** (#1) newly awarded XP (a new orb's first scan) goes into the orb it finds, which stays where it is with age 0, and an old orb's own scan keeps the scanner (R23 `xp_rules`, `xp_merge`; the zh wiki's "lower id stays" was the first fix, replaced); (#2) the level-up sound is timed by the step of the gain (`AFoundationPawn::LevelGainStep`, `FoundationExperienceOrbs::GetLevelUpStep`); (#3) one gain across several multiples of 5 plays the first (provisional reading).
- **Armor:** item fields (`Equipment`, `bSwappable`, `EquipSound`, `Armor`, `ArmorToughness`); `carved_pumpkin` on the head by id; `GetWornArmor` (each piece in its own slot), `WearArmor` (max(1, floor(damage / 4)) per piece, provisional; lab tool wear, not creative), right-click equip (`UseEquip`; R19's creative copy), `Equipped` events and the equip sound (`survival.r5.play_calls`), the vitals' worn values in the hurt path (`armor.formula`), the HUD row from `GetArmor()` (R19's geometry was already right).
