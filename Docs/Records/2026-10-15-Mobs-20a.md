# Survival 20a: the mob framework and the cow (record)

Lane `feature/survival-mobs` (base `feature/survival` 4b826e4, a fast-forward), survival track T6, run 2. Switch
`foundation.Mobs` (1, the default: mobs exist; 0: none spawn, step or draw, and Resolve sees training targets only, as
before). `foundation.Mobs.Summon [cow]` puts a cow 3 blocks ahead of the player; `foundation.Mobs.Clear` removes every
mob. Next: [the 20b handoff](2026-10-15-Mobs-Handoff-20b.md).

## 1. What was built

- **References.** `Reference/minecraft-entities-26.3` (extended through its own `tools/build_reference.py` under the PM's
  lock): the 17 adult mob textures appended to `TEXTURES` (the orb keeps index 0), the 8 mobs' and 16 sheep colours'
  entity loot tables (`LOOT`), the 4 meats' smelting recipes (`RECIPES`, the furnace_smelt results), and two facts files
  made by their tools: `facts/entity_models.json` (`tools/EntityModelFacts.java`, the scout's public-API model probe:
  every part's pivot and rotation, every cube face's corners and UVs, 19 layers) and `facts/mob_attributes.json`
  (`tools/mob_probe.py`, a headless 26.3 server's `/attribute` answers). `Reference/minecraft-items-26.3` (under the
  DA_Items lock): the 18 drops of the eight mobs appended as their own `APPEND_LAST` group `mob_drop` (items 86-103:
  string, feather, gunpowder, porkchop, cooked porkchop, leather, milk bucket, egg, bone, beef, chicken, cooked chicken,
  rotten flesh, spider eye, carrot, potato, mutton, cooked mutton); the sort is now by the group's rank in
  `APPEND_LAST`, so no earlier item, model, texture or layer moves. `DA_Items` and its two sprite arrays were moved away
  and reimported (`ImportItems.py`: 104 items; a second run verifies).
- **Loot** (`FoundationLoot`): conditions `killed_by_player` (`FFoundationLootContext::bKilledByPlayer`) and
  `entity_properties` with facts (`this`: `is_on_fire`, `sheep/color`, `sheared`; any other fact, the attacker's
  enchantments or the killer's type, fails); modifiers `furnace_smelt` (the item becomes its smelting result) and
  `enchanted_count_increase` (neutral, no Looting). Block tables roll as before. `FoundationLootAssets::MakeTable` is
  `AddLoot`'s former body, shared by `DA_Items`' block tables and `DA_Mobs`' entity tables.
- **DA_Mobs** (`UFoundationMobAssets`, `/Game/Entities/DA_Mobs`, written by `Scripts/Editor/ImportMobs.py` through
  `MobImportData.py`): the 8 mobs' main layers (parts, pivots, rotations, faces), their textures (`T_Mob_<Mob>`, stand-ins,
  `Docs/StandIns.md`), `M_Mob` (lit, masked at alpha 0.1, the red overlay from custom data 0) and the entity loot tables.
  Six tables import; zombie (a weighted pool, a `vehicle` predicate) and creeper (a `tag` entry) are left out with the
  reason logged, for 20c and 20d. The skeleton's table names `arrow`, which T2 19c brings: until then it is skipped at
  runtime (logged).
- **Mobs** (`FoundationMobs.h/.cpp`): the kind table (eight kinds; only the cow summons), `FFoundationMob` (health and the
  hurt rules through `FFoundationVitals`, timers, navigation, look, walk animation, its own random stream), physics
  (`Move`: acceleration, the move through `FFoundationSimulation::Clip` with a 0.6-block step-up, then the retention and
  gravity), `ApplyKnockback`, `OnHurt`, the AI (`Think`: panic on a hit, re-picked while the hit is recent; the stroll
  on a 1-in-120 roll; look-at-player) and steering (`Steer`: straight lines, a jump when blocked past the step-up).
- **Authority**: `SpawnMob`, `FindMob`, `ClearMobs`, `StepMobs` after `StepItemEntities` (the freeze on an unloaded
  column, the timers, the death at tick 20 with `DropMobLoot`: the entity table with `killed_by_player`, the drops as item
  entities, 1-3 experience orbs for a player kill), `Resolve` adds mob boxes, `Attack` hits a mob through the same damage
  line (charge, critical) with knockback on a fresh hit, the hurt or death sound, and the panic trigger; sweeps stay with
  the training targets. Sounds go out as `MobSound` events (hurt, death, ambient, step) and play through `DA_Sounds`.
- **View** (`FFoundationMobView`, owned by the interaction view): one transient mesh per (mob, part) from `DA_Mobs`, one
  instanced component per part, the part transforms each frame (pivot, rest rotation, leg swing, head turn), the death
  roll at the root, the red overlay in custom data.
- **Probe**: `TestFoundationRuntime.ps1 -Suite mobs` (`FoundationMobsProbe.cpp`): the cow standing (front, back, side),
  walking, hit, dying and its drops, then the `foundation.Mobs` 1/0 control pair, with shots.

## 2. Decisions

- Player damage to mobs reuses `Attack`'s damage line (charge 0.2 + 0.8q², critical): vanilla, no new code (SM, plan).
- The cow's walking was measured black-box on a 26.3 server (`C:\dev\sandbox\mobs-20a\speed_probe\summary.md`): ground
  acceleration (attribute × modifier)² blocks per tick (0.04 strolling, 0.16 panicking), plateaus 0.0881 and 0.352, the
  knockback track 0.4, 0.2184, 0.1987 … (O5), panic in 2-3 legs stopping 40-62 ticks after the hit. The code follows it.
- A dying mob still stops the aim (vanilla keeps it pickable until removed); `Attack` refuses it (`TargetDead`).
- The food table gains the 11 new foods from `items.json` (their hunger and poison effects wait for status effects).
- Values: every provisional one has a row in `C:\dev\tools\shift-managers\survival\provisional-values.md` (T6 20a).
- Look call (SM, 2026-10-15): accepted; the overlay's 0.3 stays provisional (it reads more saturated than vanilla's
  Cow_Death.png at distance).

## 4. Known issues, with when

- Zombie and creeper loot tables are not in `DA_Mobs` (weighted pools, `vehicle`, `tag` entries): 20c, 20d.
- Raw chicken, rotten flesh and spider eye eat without their effects: when status effects exist.
- Straight-line steering only (no A*), so mobs walk into walls and give up after 40 ticks: 20b.
- Look gaps: the overlay strength (compare a close-up against Cow_Death.png), head-look turns, the walk cycle's speed
  calibration: 20b.
- Player attack sounds (`entity.player.attack.*`) do not play on hits yet: 20c, with the charge and sweep work.
- The farm-animal variants (cold, warm) and the sheep's wool layers are in the reference but not drawn: 20b, 20e.
