# Survival 20c2: the spider, the monster cycle, the light proxy, despawn, Peaceful (record)

Lane `feature/survival-mobs` (base 540cfd23 = `feature/survival` after 20c1), survival track T6, run 2. Plan confirmed by

## 1. What was built

- **The monster cycle** (`RunSpawnCycle(category)`; `RunCreatureCycle` is the creature call): monsters every step
  [survival2.r20 population runs: ordinary categories every tick], cap `floor(70 x eligible / 289)` and each player's
  local cap 70 [survival.r10.spawn_caps F 5461-5470]. The census (Codex 30 #1, #2) counts every live mob of the
  category that is not frozen, globally, and each player's square without frozen mobs (the creature cycle too). A
  column's round starts at a random height from the world's bottom to one above the start column's top non-air block;
  a full solid there skips the column; the attempts keep that height [Codex 02 C, provisional]. The feet stand on a
  full block top (not leaves), with room for the box and no fluid. The round's entry is drawn from the biome's monster
  list with every 26.3 entry kept; mobs not built yet are blank and a round that draws one spawns nothing (SM decision,
  closer to vanilla's shares: plains spider 100 and zombie 90 of 515). Counters `MonsterCycles`,
  `MonstersCycleSpawned`, `MonsterCycleCount/Cap`, `MonsterCycleMsTotal/Max`; the `Mobs:` line prints them.
- **The light rule** [mobs.spawn_light V:469-471] (`RollSkyLight`, `RollMonsterLight`): the sky light above a random
  0-31 fails, any block light fails (overworld limit 0), then the light must be at most a random 0-7.
- **The light proxy** (settled; provisional-values.md T6 20c2): sky-visible = above the column's motion-blocking
  heightmap (the voxel storage's own, `IFoundationBlockStorage::GetTopZ`; other storage scans up), giving raw sky 15
  and the open-sky light of the day's `sky_light_level`; block light = the max over emitters within 15 of emission
  minus the Manhattan distance, no occlusion (`GetBlockLightProxy`), from per-section emitter lists reused 20 steps,
  skipped when the section's palette holds no emitting state (`IFoundationBlockStorage::MayHold`), at most 8 section
  scans a step on the spawn path. `IsSkyVisible` (the sun burn's) reads the heightmap too when the storage has one.
- **Despawn** [mobs.despawn; survival.r10.spawning_rules S 5472] (`ShouldDespawn`, in `StepMobs`): monsters only;
  beyond 128 blocks of every player at once; beyond 32 after `NoActionTicks` > 600 a 1 in 800 roll a step; within 32
  the idle count restarts, as does a hit. Frozen mobs are skipped; with no player nothing goes. `MobsDespawned`.
- **Peaceful** (minecraft.wiki "Difficulty"): player facts carry the world difficulty (`FFoundationPlayerFacts::
  Difficulty`, from the pawn's vitals); any player on Peaceful removes every monster on the next step and stops the
  monster cycle.
- **The spider** (`foundation.Mobs.Summon spider`): 16 / 0.3 / attack 2 / 1.4 x 0.9 eye 0.65, death roll 180 (data
  since 20a); stroll 0.8 [S 5447] (kinds gained `StrollSpeed`); SpiderTargetGoal only below brightness 0.5 at its eyes
  (light 11 and below), a target forgotten in the light 1 in 100 a step (Codex 02 C, provisional); SpiderAttackGoal
  follows an unseen target (it repaths without sight; the 60-step memory still drops it) [S 5420-5432]; the leap on the
  ground at a target 2-4 blocks away: 0.4 up [S 5423], 0.4 toward it plus 0.2 of its velocity (provisional); climbing:
  against a wall it rises 0.2 a step, falling at most 0.15 (provisional). Eight legs: yaw 0.4 x speed and roll toward
  the rest by up to 0.4 x speed added to the model data's rest pose [survival.r10.model_motion F 5264-5299: 45 -> 67.9
  degrees at speed 1; roll within 45]; per-pair phases provisional. Its loot table was already in DA_Mobs.
- **DA_Mobs** re-imported (`MobImportData.py`, `ImportMobs.py`): each biome's monster entries (56 lists, 461 monster
  entries); the re-saved textures and M_Mob were reverted (20c1's lesson).

## 4. Known issues, with when

- Cost: an under-cap monster cycle in the real world costs about 1.5 ms (worst step 3.1 ms); the mean over a night's
  1040 steps is 0.30 ms because the cap fills within ~200 steps. Spreading is the knob (QC list if it shows in frames).
- The emitter lists are reused 20 steps: a torch placed in the dark protects its spot from spawns up to a second late.
- Spiders spawn about a third as often as zombies in generated caves (52 zombies / 19 spiders at step 1040): their
  1.4-block box fits fewer cave spots; vanilla's shares are equal-ish on R20's flat platform. Watch it in 20d's runs.
- Floors: glass and other full blocks vanilla refuses for spawning are allowed (provisional row); leaves are refused.
- No creeper, skeleton, slime, enderman or witch yet (their weights are blanks): 20d and later.
- The Playground has no sky hook or biome hook: nothing spawns there, and the spider there is always in full daylight.
