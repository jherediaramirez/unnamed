## 1. What was built

- **Orbs** (`Source/Unnamed/Foundation/FoundationExperienceOrbs.h/.cpp`; `FFoundationAuthority::SpawnExperience`, `TakeExperience`, `StepExperienceOrbs`): a survival break of an ore that the tool can harvest pays orbs (coal 0-2, diamond and emerald 3-7, lapis and nether quartz 2-5, redstone 1-5, nether gold 0-1; deepslate variants alike; iron, copper and gold none). They fall, follow the nearest live player within 8 blocks, merge with same-value orbs, despawn at 6,000 steps, and a player takes one every 2 steps. The points go to `FFoundationPlayer::PendingExperience`; the pawn moves them into `FFoundationVitals::AddExperience` after each step, and the existing HUD bar (`Hud/*`, unchanged) moves from there.
- **The furnace hook:** `SpawnExperience(LocalCm, Total, Seed)` is public; T1's step 8a calls it when a furnace's output is taken. Not wired here.
- **Motion:** `FoundationItemEntities::MoveBox` is the items' old `Move` body with a gravity and a push parameter; the items call it unchanged (gravity 0.04, no push).
- **Look** (`FoundationExperienceOrbView.h/.cpp`, owned by `UFoundationInteractionView`): one instanced camera-facing quad per orb on `M_ExperienceOrb` (custom data: icon, the pulse's red and blue), full bright. Sound hooks `entity.experience_orb.pickup` and `entity.player.levelup` (every 5th level) are called and stay silent until T5's sound batch.
- **Assets:** `Reference/minecraft-entities-26.3/` (new hash-checked reference: `tools/build_reference.py` reads the pinned client jar, `verify_reference.py`, `SHA256SUMS.txt`, `textures.json`; only `entity/experience/experience_orb.png`, append-only for T6). `UUnnamedProjectSettings::EntityRoot` (`/Game/Entities`, cooked), `Scripts/Editor/CreateEntityAssets.py` (imports `T_ExperienceOrb`, builds `M_ExperienceOrb`). `Docs/StandIns.md` (new) has the orb's row.
- **The switch:** `foundation.Items.Xp` (default 1; `Options.bExperienceOrbs`). At 0 no orbs spawn.
- **Swaps (S8b-a, S8b-b, a separate hunk):** the item merge cadence is 2 steps when the item's integer cell changed that step, else 40 (`items.physics`); the merge box no longer grows up or down (`items.merge_boundary_stationary`). The throw, despawn and copy values are marked sourced (numbers unchanged).
