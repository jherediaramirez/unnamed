## 1. What was built

- **Behaviour** (`Docs/Items.md`, "Item entities"): a survival break's loot pops out of the block as item entities that fall, rest on the ground, bob and spin, and are pulled into the inventory when the player walks over them, after a 10-step delay. Only what fits transfers; the rest stays. A full inventory no longer refuses a break: the drop waits on the ground. `foundation.Items.Entities` (default 1); 0 returns the loot straight to the inventory and refuses a break that does not fit, exactly as before (the old code path, kept whole behind `Options.bItemEntities`).
- **The authority owns the drops** (`FoundationItemEntities.h/.cpp`, `FFoundationAuthority`): plain data stepped in `Step()` after the targets' cooldowns: motion (`FoundationItemEntities::Move`, through `FFoundationSimulation::Clip`, the player's own sweep), the pickup delay, then each live player's pickups (`PickedUp` events). `BreakBlock` spawns each rolled stack (split at its max stack) under the option. `SpawnItem` is public (tests, probes, step 4's Q throw). `Reset` clears the drops. Positions are cm from the block world's origin.
- **Collision and streaming:** the new `FFoundationAuthority::GatherCollision` hook, which the Playground and the generated world set to their own `GatherCollision`, the query the pawn moves against (in a generated world ungenerated columns are solid, so a drop stops at their face as the player does). Unset, the block world's boxes and the statics. A drop in a column that is not loaded is frozen (no motion, age, delay or pickup) and hidden until the column is back; below the world's lowest cell minus 64 it vanishes.
- **The switch:** `FFoundationLabOptions::bItemEntities` defaults to false, so every test-built authority keeps the inventory path; `AFoundationPawn::StepInteraction` copies the console variable in before each step.
- **The look** (`FoundationItemEntityView.h/.cpp`, owned by `UFoundationInteractionView` like the break effects): the held item's meshes in the models' `ground` display, drawn between steps with the provisional bob and spin; block items on the block materials, other items as their extruded sprite on a new `M_HeldItem` instance without the hand's glow (lit by the scene; `UFoundationInteractionView::MakeItemMaterial`). The held view's block-part and sprite-mesh code moved into shared helpers (`GetStateMeshes`, `GetLayerMaterial`, `GetItemMesh`) without changing what the hand draws. A whole stack picked up flies to the player over 3 steps.
- **The pickup sound's hook:** the `PickedUp` case in the view's `HandleEvents` (`FFoundationItemEntityView::OnPickedUp`), a no-op with a comment until the sound reference's `FFoundationBreakEffects::PlayEvent` is merged (the manager's plan: wired in the merge pass).
- **Also:** the controller's interaction log names the new event ("picked up 1 cobblestone"; its name table gained a `static_assert` against the enum after a crash, section 2). `FoundationSimulation.cpp`'s anonymous `Epsilon` is now `SimulationEpsilon`: the new files regrouped the unity blobs and it collided with an engine header's parameter name. `CaptureVoxel.ps1 -Drops` (with `-Caves`) drops a cobblestone and a coal before each cave view and looks down at them.

## 2. Decisions

- **The manager's go (October 13):** the plan as proposed; Q1 default 1; Q2 the capture pair plus one cave or night frame; Q3 no mip reimport (a known issue judged from the capture); Q4 extra copies for bigger stacks wait for step 4; Q5 the four deferred gaps listed below; the sound hook a no-op until the sound reference merges.
- **Constants:** sourced from minecraft.wiki ("Item (entity)": the 0.25 box, the 10-tick block-drop delay, the pickup box, partial pickups, the void; "Entity", Motion table: gravity 0.04, drag 0.98 on both axes, terminal velocity 1.96, which puts the acceleration before the drag). Provisional until probe P3 (no vanilla client run without Jesus's go): the rest of the order in a step, the ground friction 0.6, the spawn spread and speed, the strict pickup edge, and the look (lift 0.25 x the ground scale, bob, spin, the 3-step fly). See `Docs/Items.md`.
- **Pickup order:** drops step before pickups, so the earliest pickup is the 10th step after the spawn; vanilla's exact tick order is unverified.
- **The Sonnet 5.5 review** (the diff only; `C:\dev\sandbox\items-s8a\review\s8a-1.diff`): nothing severe. Fixed: (1) no test moved the world origin (`.Origin`); (2) a drop whose look failed to set up (assets not loaded) was never retried (`Visual.Item` reset on failure); (6) a pickup merging into the held stack bumped its revision, so a break in progress was canceled (`ItemChanged`) and restarted, as digging dirt by hand with dirt selected would do every block: `FFoundationInventory::Add`'s new `bTouchStacks` (false for pickups) and `.HeldStack`. Checked, no change: (5) the generated world sets `BoundsMin.Z` to its floor; (4) `Owned` is a `UPROPERTY` and is never emptied while the view lives; (7) no finite case after 13a counts returns; (9) the cave-shot spawn runs at the shot's first frame only. Left: (3) the control frame does not itself prove switch 0 (scenario 13 does, by its inventory count); (8) the controller's log shows identical consecutive pickups once (its existing repeat filter).
- **The capture pair's hold:** the probe starts the next case in the same tick as a case's check, so a capture followed by a lab reset lost its drops before the frame was shot. Both drop captures get a 0.2 s hold case after them.
