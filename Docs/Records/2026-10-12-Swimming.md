## What changed
- `FFoundationEligibility` (`FoundationSimulation.h`): `EFoundationFluid Fluid` (None/Water/Lava), `bEyesInFluid`, `FluidAtFeetCm`, `IsFluidFree` (the jump-out test). Left empty they change nothing.
- `FFoundationSimulation::Step` (`FoundationSimulation.cpp`), in fluid and not flying:
  - acceleration `SwimAccelerationCmPerTick` (no sprint ×1.3, no ground/air split; the sneak slowdown stays);
  - no ground jump unless `FluidAtFeetCm < ShallowJumpCm`;
  - `Vz += (Jump − Sneak) × SwimVerticalCmPerTick`;
  - after the move, instead of gravity and friction: `V *= drag` on every axis (water `.8`, `.9` sprinting with the eyes under; lava `.5`), then `Vz −= ` the fluid's gravity (`.5` / `2` cm);
  - jump-out: a horizontal collision where the body raised `JumpOutRaiseCm` (plus the blocked horizontal move) fits and is fluid-free sets `Vz = JumpOutCmPerTick`.
- `FoundationProfile.h`: `SwimAccelerationCmPerTick` 2, `WaterDrag` .8, `WaterSprintDrag` .9, `WaterGravityCmPerTick` .5, `LavaDrag` .5, `LavaGravityCmPerTick` 2 (probe); `SwimVerticalCmPerTick` 4, `ShallowJumpCm` 40, `JumpOutRaiseCm` 60, `JumpOutCmPerTick` 30 (unverified).
- `AFoundationLab` (`FoundationLab.h/.cpp`): `GetFluid(point)`, `GetFluid(box)`, `GetFluidAboveCm(feet)`, `GetFluidFacts(bSwim, ...)` (fills the eligibility facts; with `bSwim` false it clears them). The Playground answers from its water boxes.
- `AVoxelWorld` (`VoxelWorld.h/.cpp`): `GetFluidBox(cell, box, &bLava)` (lava = any held fluid whose block isn't `water`); `GetWaterBox` now calls it and keeps its meaning (water and lava). `GetFluid` and `GetFluidAboveCm` overrides.
- `FoundationPawn.cpp`: CVar `foundation.Swim`; before each `Simulation.Step`, `Lab->GetFluidFacts(Swim != 0, ...)`. With Swim 1 the vitals' body/eyes-in-water facts use `GetFluid == Water` (lava no longer drowns or slows mining); with 0 they use `IsInWater` as before.
- Probe: `-FoundationSuite=swim` (`FoundationSwimProbe.cpp`, not part of `all`): the control pair in the Playground pool.
- Docs: `Docs/Foundation/Behavior.md` (step order, a swimming paragraph).
