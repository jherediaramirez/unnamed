## What 8b1 built

- **Codex review 18 #1, the switch:** with `foundation.Furnace 0`, a furnace, smoker or blast furnace has no use (`UseFurnace` returns false; `WouldUseFurnace` agrees), so a held block places against it and no entity is made, as before 8a. With the switch at 1, the use opens the block's own menu. The cvar, `FFoundationLabOptions::bFurnace` and Items.md now say so.
- **The lit state:** after each tick, `StepFurnaces` sets the block's `lit` to the entity's `IsLit()`, on the same tick (`survival.r3.furnace_ticks`, F 3114-3121: lit at tick 1, unlit at fuel + 1). It uses `WithValue` and `World.Set`.
  - The block id is unchanged, so the entity stays.
  - The flip is a plain state change, like an axe's strip. The renderers re-mesh and re-light the cell. No `Placed` event, so no place sound.
  - The only `OnSet` hook is the fluid system's; a furnace flip gives it nothing to do.
- **The smoker and blast furnace:** same entity kind, layout and tick. The cooking kind comes from the block (`FoundationFurnace::GetCookingKind`).
  - Speed 2 (`survival.r3.fuels`, `survival.r3.recipes`):
    - fuel uses the data's own smoker and blast columns (`GetBurnTicks(Item, Kind)`; coal 800);
    - the recipes' loaded 200 ticks cook in 100 (`GetCookTotal`; `GetSpeed`).
  - Shift-clicks route by the open block (`MakeContainerRules(Block)`; `survival.r3.shift_click`, F 3219-3234).
  - New menu kinds `Smoker` and `BlastFurnace`, with `IsFurnaceMenu` in every table indexed by the kind: the layout, slot positions, recipe button, background and art, title, and the event-log name.
- **Screens:** the furnace's layout (value-swaps.md "SmokerScreen", "BlastFurnaceScreen"; `survival.r3.sprites`).
  - Each has its own background, flame and arrow (`GetFurnaceArt`).
  - Titles "Smoker" and "Blast Furnace", in the furnace's provisional place.
  - 6 new GUI textures of our own (`ImportContainerGui.py`: `CONTAINER_GUI_IMPORT PASS created=6 verified=15`) and 6 `Docs/StandIns.md` rows.
- **Sounds** (`foundation.Furnace.Ambience`, default 1): `UFoundationInteractionView::PlayFurnaceSounds`.
  - While lit, within 16 blocks of the local pawn, at the block's bottom centre, volume 1, pitch 1, each block plays its own sound:
    - furnace: `block.furnace.fire_crackle`;
    - smoker: `block.smoker.smoke`;
    - blast furnace: `block.blastfurnace.fire_crackle` (event 332).
  - Each display callback plays the sound with chance 0.1, volume 1, pitch 1 (`survival2.r15.exact_callback_chances`, `survival2.r15.sounds`, SURVIVAL-RUN2-FINDINGS R15).
  - The callbacks' scheduling is provisional: one on 1 tick in 50 per lit block (R15: client-spatial, not a constant).
  - No DA_Sounds change.
- **Codex review 22 #1:** the shift-double-click release bulk-moves like stacks from the clicked slot's side only (`FHudContainerModel::MouseUp`). Logs shift-clicked out of an input no longer route straight back in.
- **Tour** (case n becomes a loop over the three blocks, plus case o): shots `24e-furnace`, `24f-smoker`, `24g-blast-furnace` and `24h-lit-furnace` (a coal-burning furnace facing the camera shows `furnace_front_on`).
- **Tests:**
  - `Unnamed.Foundation.Furnace.Switch0`: off places planks against all three blocks with no entity; on opens each block's kind.
  - `Unnamed.Foundation.Furnace.Speed`, for each block:
    - the coal burn boundaries 1600/800/800 with cook 200/100/100: lit at tick 1, the block lit on the same tick, no `Placed` event, the same id and entity, the first output at the cook total, 8 outputs, unlit at fuel + 1;
