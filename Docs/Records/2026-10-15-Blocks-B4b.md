## 1. What changed

- **Twisting and weeping vines (first item):** `FoundationBlockFamilies::GetSurvival` gives them a rule (before the
  `foundation.Blocks.Interact` gate): twisting vines and their body need below them, weeping vines and theirs above
  them, the same vine or a sturdy face toward them (`family_survival.json` signatures 89 and 88).
  `FoundationPlantRules::GetStack` adds both as stacks, so a broken support pops the column and B4a's pop drops apply.
- **Ladder** (`EFoundationBlockFamily::Ladder`): placement by the first side look where it survives, facing away from
  that wall; a ladder clicked on a same-facing ladder's front face places nothing; survival on the sturdy face behind
  it. Measured by `Tests/Probes/LadderPlacementProbe.java` (a copy of the switch probe's parts A and B, plus part D;
  2,730 public `useOn` placements, no anomalies, two runs byte-identical, SHA-256 `A8740D36…13FCDB51`).
- **Climbing** (`foundation.Blocks.Climb`, default 1): `FoundationBlockFamilies::IsClimbable` (`Block.Climbable`, or an
  open trapdoor over a same-facing ladder) at the feet cell sets `FFoundationEligibility::bOnClimbable` in the pawn.
  `FFoundationSimulation::Step` (T4's file; gated edits only) clamps each horizontal axis and the descent to 15 cm a
  tick, holds on sneak, and sets 20 cm a tick up on a wall push or jump before gravity and drag; `bClimbing` zeroes
  `FallDistanceCm` there and, through `FFoundationVitalsStep::bClimbing`, in `FFoundationVitals::StepHazards` (T4's
  file); the pawn passes it to the critical-hit facts. 26.3's climbable tag also lists scaffolding, which is not an
  imported block (`block_manifest.json`'s 8 tagged blocks are the rest of it).
- **R13:** `Scripts/MakeR13Oracles.py` adds the 8 ladder states (`BlockR13Oracles.json` regenerated by the script,
  3,624 shape rows). R13's vines list the same boxes in another order than `block_state_facts.json` (checked: the last
  two of five swapped), so they stay out.
- **Docs:** `Blocks.md` "Ladders, climbing and vines (B4b)" and the tests; `Breaking.md` the switch row;
  `Tests/Probes/README.md`. Provisional rows (`provisional-values.md`): the ascent 0.2, the feet cell, the trapdoor rule,
  sneaking while moving.
