- **G8** (interaction suite with sound on and the trace, `runs\sound-prime0-1`, `loop-a-1024x1\run`, `sound-b256x2-2`; stats `loopback\trace_stats.ps1`):
  - A file's first play is no later than a repeat (play call to the first playback callback 32.2-32.9 ms for both; the waves load on demand), so stream caching is not the delay, and the priming fix was dropped.
  - That 32.8 ms is unchanged at 256-frame blocks: it is two game frames of callback delivery, not audio, so the in-game trace cannot see the device's latency.
  - A sound plays in the frame its block changes; a placement waits for the next reference step (up to 50 ms), as vanilla's tick does.
  - The saving is computed from the buffer sizes (about 20 ms of WASAPI buffer and 16 ms of mixer block), not measured end to end: the loopback of the default output, "Headphones (Crusher ANC 2)", recorded pure silence.
  - Load check: a flight with sound on at 256 x 2 (`runs\flight-sound-256x2-1`): one underrun at startup (frame 0), none in 9,103 frames.
- **G9** (fit `g9fit\results.json`, P1 bboxes `p1\p1_bboxes.json`; views suite `runs\views-g9-1`, 63 PASS / 0 FAIL):
