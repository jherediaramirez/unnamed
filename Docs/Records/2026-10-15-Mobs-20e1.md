# Survival T6 20e1: sheep colours and dyeing, shearing, milking, cold and warm variants

Lane `feature/survival-mobs`, after 20c2 (base 8d16cc8e = `feature/survival`). The plan is
`C:\dev\tools\shift-managers\survival\tracks\T6-plan-20e.md` (the SM's split: 20e1 here, 20e2 breeding and babies next).
`foundation.Mobs.Farm` (default 1; authority option `bMobFarm`, copied by the pawn) turns it all on; 0 is 20c2 exactly
(white unshearable sheep, temperate animals, a use on a mob passes, a sheep kill drops no wool).

## 1. What changed

- **Sheep colours** [minecraft.wiki "Sheep", provisional]: a spawned sheep rolls its colour from its own stream
  (`FoundationMobFarm::MakeSeed`: mob id, loot seed, "farm"; `Mob.Random` and the spawn streams are untouched, so the
  20b-20c tests keep their draws): black, gray, light gray 5% each, brown 3%, pink 0.164%, white the rest. The view tints
  the wool and the undercoat (`M_Mob`'s new per-instance tint, custom data 1-3) with the dye's colour x 0.75 (white
  E6E6E6; provisional, a look call). The undercoat (`sheep#wool_undercoat`, now in DA_Mobs) draws on a coloured sheep,
  shorn or not; the wool hides when shorn.
- **Shearing** [jar `loot_table/shearing/sheep.json` + 16 colour tables, now in the entities reference and DA_Mobs as
  `shearing/sheep`]: shears on a sheep with its wool drop 1-3 wool of its colour 1 block above its feet, play
  `entity.sheep.shear`, wear 1 (none in creative) and mark it shorn; a shorn sheep passes. Eating grass regrows the wool
  (`EatGrass`). A sheep kill rolls `entities/sheep.json` with its colour and shorn state: one wool of its colour unless
  shorn.
- **Dyeing** [minecraft.wiki "Dye", provisional]: a dye on a sheep with its wool and another colour recolours it and is
  used up (kept in creative); silent (`item.dye.use` is not in the sound reference).
- **Milking** [minecraft.wiki "Cow", provisional]: an empty bucket on a cow becomes a milk bucket with step 17's swap
  rules (`SwapHeldItem`, hoisted from `UseBucket` unchanged) and `entity.cow.milk`.
- **Variants** [jar `cow_variant`, `pig_variant`, `chicken_variant` and the biome tags `spawns_cold_variant_farm_animals`
  / `spawns_warm_variant_farm_animals`, #is_* members expanded]: a cow, pig or chicken spawned in a tagged biome is cold or
  warm (the biome at its feet, `GetBiomeId`). Cold cow, warm cow, cold pig and cold chicken draw their own geometry
  (`cold_cow#main` etc., appended to the reference's `facts/entity_models.json`); the warm pig and chicken keep the main
  geometry with their own texture. DA_Mobs models carry the variant ids (`cold_cow`, `warm_pig`, ...;
  `FoundationMobFarm::GetModelId`); the view keeps one draw per type and variant.
- **Commands**: `UseEntity` now takes the player mode and, for a mob, calls `FFoundationAuthority::UseOnMob` (defined in
  `FoundationMobFarm.cpp` so the hot file only calls it). Counters `MobShears`, `MobMilks`, `MobDyes`.
