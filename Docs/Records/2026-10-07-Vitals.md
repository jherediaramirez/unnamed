# Player vitals — HUD milestone 2, first slice

Project: `C:\dev\projects\Unnamed\Unnamed.uproject`, Unreal 5.8.3. Follows [HUD milestone 1](2026-10-07-Hud.md), which stays unchanged as the historical record.

This slice reviews the milestone-1 HUD code and gives the HUD its first real gameplay owner: health, absorption, armor, food, saturation and exhaustion, air, falls, experience, attack recovery, player mode and difficulty. They follow the pinned Java 26.3 rules and run on the Foundation's existing 20 Hz reference tick. Everything is session-only, single-player and limited to the Foundation lab. ## Review of milestone 1

The milestone-1 code is sound: pure layout/selection, history, draw construction and the passive view are cleanly separated and well tested, and painting never touches gameplay. Findings:

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| `LogUnnamedHud` was a file-static category in `HudPresenter.cpp`, although the naming conventions list it as the Hud area's shared category | Moved to `UnnamedLog.h/.cpp` |
| The `PREVIEW` label showed whenever any fixture data was on screen, so it could not distinguish fixture resources from the token hotbar | `PREVIEW` for the resource fixture, `PREVIEW ITEMS` when only the token hotbar is a stand-in |

| One Slate layer per draw prevents batching but guarantees painter's order for overlapping 9×9 cells | Kept deliberately |
| `UHudPresenter` combines asset loading, sampling and about 120 lines of preview-panel Slate | Noted: moving the panel out is the natural next split |
| `HudRuntimeProbe.cpp` implements a `UFoundationRuntimeProbe` method from the Hud folder and hard-codes 85 textures | Noted: a documented exception, not changed |

## What is real now

`FFoundationVitals` (`Source/Unnamed/Foundation/FoundationVitals.h`) is a pure struct owned by `AFoundationPawn` and stepped once per reference tick, right after each movement step. The pawn now asks `FFoundationSimulation::TakeDueSteps` how many steps are due and runs them itself, so each step's eligibility comes from the vitals the previous step left. `Advance` keeps its behaviour for existing callers. Floats mirror Java's types.

| Value | Rules | Driven by |
|---|---|---|
| Health, maximum health | Hurt path: invulnerability, difficulty scaling (mob hits only), 20-tick damage cooldown where a hit in the first half deals only its excess, armor, absorption, exhaustion; heal clamp; death at 0 and respawn at the lab origin after 20 ticks | Falls, drowning, starvation, F3+F6 actions |
| Absorption | Consumed before health | F3+F6 grant (no effect timer) |
| Armor points, toughness | `damage × (1 − clamp(armor − damage ÷ (2 + toughness ÷ 4), armor ÷ 5, 20) ÷ 25)`; bypassed by generic, fall, drown, starve and kill damage | F3+F6 values (equipment stand-in) |
| Food, saturation, exhaustion | Above 4 exhaustion spends 1 saturation, then 1 food; saturated full food heals min(saturation, 6) ÷ 6 every 10 ticks; food ≥ 18 heals 1 every 80 ticks for 6 exhaustion; empty food starves every 80 ticks down to 10 (Easy), 1 (Normal) or death (Hard) | Real movement: ground sprint 0.1 per block, jump 0.05, sprint jump 0.2, water 0.01 per block; damage by type; regeneration |
| Sprint eligibility | Food above 6, or a mode that may fly | Feeds the existing `FFoundationEligibility.bEnoughFood` |
| Air | −1 per tick with eyes in water; −20 drowns for 2 and resets to 0; +4 per tick out of water; Creative/Spectator neither lose nor regain air underwater | Lab water column |
| Falls | `floor(blocks + 1e-6 − 3)`; only downward movement counts; water cancels the fall | Real simulation movement |
| Experience | 7 + 2L, 37 + 5(L − 15), 112 + 9(L − 30) points per level; progress carries; negative points step back; lost on death | F3+F6 XP button (no orbs) |
| Attack recovery | One per tick (also while dead); delay 20 ÷ attack speed; restarts on an entity swing, a swing at nothing, an abandoned break and a different main-hand item | Real attack action and eye target |
| Player mode | Survival, Creative, Adventure, Spectator: invulnerability, flight-mode sprint rule, HUD policy | F3+F6 |
| Difficulty | Easy, Normal, Hard: starvation floor and mob-hit scaling | F3+F6 |

The HUD presenter now reads these by default (`EHudDataSource::Authoritative`), together with the eye-ray target: an entity fixture in reach is the crosshair entity (ready marker), and an interactive target in reach stands in for a menu target for the Spectator crosshair. The hotbar remains the token test double, so the screen shows **PREVIEW ITEMS**. The milestone-1 fixture is still available: F3+F6 → *Show the preview fixture instead of the player's vitals* (starting a timed preview sequence enables it).

Sources: the pinned 26.3 decompiled sources from the HUD research (`FoodData`, `LivingEntity`, `Player`, `MultiPlayerGameMode`), plus 26.3 data read directly from the hash-identified client jar: damage-type exhaustion and scaling, the `bypasses_armor`, `bypasses_invulnerability`, `is_fall` and `is_drowning` tags, and the constants in `FoodConstants` and `CombatRules`. The C++ is newly written; no decompiled code is copied. `ServerPlayer` is not in the decompiled set, so the order inside one tick is a documented choice: timers, movement and falls, air, then food.

## In the lab

- **Jump stair** east of the steps: four one-block stairs up to a five-block platform. Walking off deals 2 points (1 heart); jumping off about 3.
- **Water column** (3×3×3 blocks, south-east): stand in it and the bubbles drain; after about 15 s you drown for one heart per second. Leave it and the bubbles refill. There is no swimming physics; it is opaque from outside and casts no shadow.
- **F3** (Movement section): a vitals line and the last damage. The event log records each damage, death and respawn.
- **F3+F6 → PLAYER VITALS**: mode, difficulty, natural regeneration, editable values and actions (mob hit, damage, heal, eat cooked beef, exhaustion, XP, absorption, kill, restore). Values apply at once; timed rules run when the panel closes.
- Falling off the lab still teleports to spawn without void damage.

## Remaining boundaries

- No status effects (poison, wither, regeneration, hunger, resistance, absorption and its timer), Hardcore or Peaceful. These HUD states remain preview-only.
- Creative and Spectator change damage, food, air and HUD policy only; flight, noclip, the F3+F4 switcher and the spectator hotbar are later movement/UI work.
- No equipment, durability, item attributes, food items or eating time; armor, toughness and attack speed are lab values. No XP orbs.
- Death respawns automatically after one second at the lab origin; there is no death screen, keep-inventory rule or spawn point.
- No void damage, swimming physics, bubble columns or Respiration. No knockback, hurt camera tilt, sounds or particles.
- Session-only, single-player state: no save, server authority or multiplayer.
- Float arithmetic mirrors Java's types; compiler floating-point settings may still differ in the last bit.
