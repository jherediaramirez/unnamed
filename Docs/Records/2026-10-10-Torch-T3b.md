## 2. What changed

- **MegaLights:** `Config/DefaultEngine.ini` sets `r.MegaLights.EnableForProject=1` under `[/Script/Engine.RendererSettings]`.
- **Light defaults:** `voxel.LightIntensity` 600 and `voxel.LightTemperature` 2,200 (`FVoxelLights::FSettings`, which the console variables now start from). `voxel.LightFalloff` stays 0.
- **The flame cut:** `FoundationBlockMeshes::GetDrawnModelQuads`. Both worlds now draw a model's quads through it, `VoxelBlocks.cpp` `CopyModels` for the voxel table and `UFoundationInteractionView::GetBlockMesh` for the Playground.
  - It returns the registry's baked quads, except that each quad textured `torch` is cut at UV v = 8/16. That is the line between torch.png's flame (rows 6-7: ffd800, ff8f00, ffff97, ffffff) and its stick (rows 8-15, averaging sRGB 110/87/55).
  - The cut keeps each piece's corners in their slots, so winding and corner order hold.
  - The flame piece, and the top face (v 6/16 to 8/16), carry element light 1 (`TorchFlameLight`); the stick and the bottom keep the baked 0.
  - The registry, the reference and every other model are untouched.
  - The voxel mesher and the `M_Block*` materials already emit the brighter of the state's light and the element light, so neither changed.
- **The torch-rule states:** the torch rule covers 5 states, torch and the 4 wall-torch facings: models 303 and 304-307, 6 quads each, all textured `torch`. The importer sets a wall block only for the support table's measured pair (`BlockImportData.py`: the coral fans wait). There is no soul, copper or redstone torch in the registry. `Voxel.Lights` has a row per torch-rule state.
- **Playground glow:** `FoundationBlockMeshes::GetDrawnLight` gives torch-rule instances `voxel.TorchGlow` (custom data 8), and every other state keeps its own light. `voxel.TorchGlow` now applies in both worlds.
- **The default glow:** `voxel.TorchGlow` defaults to 1 (`FVoxelLights::DefaultTorchGlow`). This is warm A as Jesus saw it, not flame-only; see section 3.
- **Tests:**
  - `Voxel.Lights` gained 11 rows (75 to 86):
    - the defaults;
    - only the 5 torch models are cut, 4 quads more each, covering the same area;
    - each cut's pieces share its two corners exactly;
    - the standing torch is cut 8/16 high;
    - one row per torch-rule state: glow 0 gives 5 flame quads at 1, 5 stick quads at 0 and nothing else, and the Playground draws it at `voxel.TorchGlow`;
    - the torch rule covers 5 states;
    - every other state draws at its own light.
  - `Voxel.LightPool` gained a defaults row (43 to 44).
  - `Blocks.ModelMeshes` and `Mesher.ModelData` take the new quad counts.
- **Text:** `CaptureVoxel.ps1`'s `-TorchGlow` note and `look.txt`'s glow note.

## 3. The look: why the stick is tan for now

The final pair, all captured with `CaptureVoxel.ps1 -TorchLook` on this build:
- **today:** `-TorchGlow 14 -ExecCmds 'voxel.LightIntensity 1000,voxel.LightTemperature 3000'`;
- **warm A, whole torch glowing:** `-TorchGlow 1`, which is now the default;
- **flame only:** glow 0, with the flame at 1.

All three runs use the same seed, room, cameras and frozen exposure (0.0078125, the auto-exposure floor). Each shot is taken with MegaLights 1 and 0. All 24 frames pass the validity check: none is black, saturated or empty.

| Crop (1.2 blocks from the torch) | Mean luma ML1 / ML0 | Saturated % ML1 | The stick's mean sRGB (box 730,330 to 860,820) |
|---|---|---|---|
| today (glow 14) | 98.5 / 55.2 | 5.12 | 251/246/231: white |
| warm A, whole torch (glow 1, the default) | 69.6 / 37.8 | 1.03 | 202/176/126: tan, textured, about 1.8 times the texture |
| flame only (glow 0) | 56.7 / 24.2 | 1.07 | 9/3/1: near-black |

The room with one torch, MegaLights on, reads 43.6, 32.9 and 32.6 for the three.

**Control:** today's and warm A's numbers repeat T3's look pack (crop ML1 98.48 against 98.49, and 69.57 against 69.54). So the switch took effect and nothing else moved.

**Why the flame-only stick is black:**
- The torch's quads are lit as up (vanilla's `shade: false`).
- The torch's own light hangs 1/16 block above its 2 x 2 top face, so that face shadows the whole stick from the only direction the stick takes light.
- The floor's bounce comes from below and doesn't count.
- Unshadowed, the stick would be blown out (600 cd from about 0.3 block away).

So no lighting change gives a brown stick. Only a glow under level 1 can, and glow steps are whole levels (level 1 is vertex G 17).

**The call (the manager's, under Jesus's away rule): B.**
- The default is glow 1, warm A as Jesus saw it: the stick is tan and textured, and the flame glows.
- The black stick would fail his "you should still see the texture under it".
- The flame cut stays in place, so flame-only is one switch away (`voxel.TorchGlow 0`).
- The exact brown stick is T3c: a glow under level 1 for the stick quads, after S9b's commit, because it touches `VoxelMesher.cpp`. The route and sweep are in [the handoff](2026-10-10-Torch-Handoff-T4.md).
