## 1. What was built

- **The zombie** (`FoundationMobs.cpp` MobKinds; `foundation.Mobs.Summon zombie`): 20 health, movement 0.23, attack 3,
  armor 2, 0.6 x 1.95 (eye 1.74) [mobs.base_attributes, catalog.entities]; follow range 35 [survival.r10.ai_rules];
  look distance 8 [survival.r10.ai_defaults S 5388]; en_us.json "Zombie". Kinds gained `AttackDamage`,
  `FollowRangeCm`, `LookCm`, `Name`; `IsMonster`.
- **Hostile AI** (`ThinkHostile`, before `Think`): the nearest targetable player (Survival or Adventure, alive) in the
  follow range that it sees, searched on even steps with a 1 in 5 roll [S 5398 randomInterval 5]; kept while in range
  and seen within 60 steps [unseenMemoryTicks 60]; a player's hit makes that player the target (HurtByTargetGoal). The
  melee goal [S 5392: attackInterval 20, speedModifier 1] chases (A*, a repath delay), looks at the target, and hits
  when the cooldown is out, the target is in reach and in sight. No stroll while it has a target.
- **Line of sight** (`HasMobLineOfSight`): a grid walk from eye to eye; any collision box (`GatherMobBoxes`: the world
  owner's collision, so ungenerated columns block) on the segment hides the player.
- **Hits on the player**: `StepMonster` queues a `FFoundationMobHit` (mob, damage, name, where it stood) on the player
  (`FFoundationPlayer::MobHits`, reset each `StepMobs`); the pawn takes them after `Authority.Step()` (`TakeMobHits`)
  and calls `Vitals.Hurt(MobAttack, 3, "Zombie")`. Difficulty, armor, the damage cooldown and death are the vitals'
  own. A fresh hit (not inside the cooldown's first half) knocks the player back with the mobs' rule: 0.4 away, 0.4 up
  grounded [extra.motion, movement.knockback_resistance; survival2.r20.mob_physics]. Player facts carry the game mode.
- **Death message**: `FFoundationVitals::Hurt` takes an optional attacker name: "Player was slain by Zombie"
  (death.attack.mob; 11a's TODO). Callers without a name are unchanged.
- **Sun burn** (`StepSunBurn`, the zombie only, with `foundation.Fire`): the day timeline's `gameplay/monsters_burn`
  and `gameplay/sky_light_level` (the sky reference, data) through `FFoundationAuthority::GetSkyFacts`, set by
  `AVoxelWorld::SetupAuthority` at the clock's time of day; brightness f / (4 - 3f) of the open-sky light
  (`VoxelSky::OpenSkyLight`); above 0.5 the roll U x 30 < (brightness - 0.4) x 2; then the eye cell must see the sky
  (`IsSkyVisible`: no collision box or fluid above) and the body be out of water: 160 fire steps
  [survival.r10.idle_and_sun S 5457]. Unset hook (the Playground): no burning.
- **Zombie loot** (`MobImportData.py`, DA_Mobs re-imported): the vehicle predicate is a fact nothing has (false); a
  pool only a rider passes (the chicken-jockey disc) is left out; the rare pool's three plain entries are a weighted
  choice (new `Weight` on loot candidates: `FoundationLoot.h/.cpp`, `FoundationItemAssets.h/.cpp`; 0 keeps the old
  first-that-passes for every existing table); furnace_smelt of potato from `FoundationCookingData.inl` (the same 26.3
  jar's recipes) since the entity reference carries only the meat recipes. Creeper stays out (its tag entry, 20d).
- **View**: the zombie's arms point ahead and dip over a 6-step swing after a hit (provisional).

## 2. Decisions

- Split and scope: SM, 2026-10-15. Rain and door breaking out (noted). The sky facts come from the sky reference's
  timeline (data); the light at the eyes is the settled proxy (open sky where the sky is visible, else 0 until 20c2's
  block light).
- No new event kind: the hit queue is enough (zombies have no attack sound; the player's hurt sound is 11b's).

## 4. Known issues, with when

- Zombie hurt tilt has no direction (11b's tilt is direction-less for every cause): T4.
- HurtByTarget's alert of nearby zombies, baby zombies, reinforcements, door breaking, rain stopping the burn, a helmet:
  later steps.
- The reach, target cadence and height, repath delay and arm pose are provisional (provisional-values.md, T6 20c1).
- The Playground has no sky hook: zombies never burn there.
- **A\* finds no path on the Playground's floor**: its collision query reports tall boxes (to 5 blocks) in open-ground
  cells near the probe spot (`GetPathCell` there: below 6.0, here 5.0, above 2.0), so a chasing zombie stands still
  (the probe summons it in reach). Generated worlds and the native lab path normally. Since 20b (tempt and stroll
  there plan the same way): 20c2 looks at the Playground's `GatherCollision`.
- Packaged builds read the sky reference from the project folder; without it monsters see no day (a warning).
