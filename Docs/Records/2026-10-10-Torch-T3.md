## 1. What changed

### Placement by look (probe P3, commit `03a69d1`)

- **The probe:** `Tests/Probes/TorchPlacementProbe.java` drives an in-process 26.3 server through its public API only (`MinecraftServer.spin` with `GameTestServer.create`, and a `ServerPlayer` without a connection calling `ItemStack.useOn`).
  - It made 64,896 torch clicks: 208 support configs times 312 looks (yaw 0-345 by 15°, pitch -90 to 90 by 15°).
  - Case A clicks every face of stone, the underside included, for every on/off set of floor and wall supports. Case B clicks every face of short grass.
  - There were 0 anomalies. The matrix `torch-placement-26.3.json` (SHA-256 `5B45941B...F7EAD5E2`) is byte-identical on a re-run, including from the repo copy (`run-torch-placement-probe.ps1`).
  - `check_rule.py` reproduces every entry from the look table and the config.
- **The rule, as measured:**
  - Start from vanilla's nearest-looking order. On a solid click, the direction into the clicked block moves to the front; on a replaceable click the order is unchanged.
  - Skip up. Down gives the floor torch if the cell below holds one.
  - A side gives the wall torch on the first side in that order that holds one, facing away from it. This is the first holding side in the whole order, even one listed after down.
  - Nothing holding means nothing is placed.
  - Side clicks therefore always hang the torch on the clicked block, and top clicks always stand it, as in T2.
- **The code:**
  - `FoundationBlockPlacement::GetTorchState` and `GetClickLooks` replace `GetItemBlock` and the underside refusal.
  - `GetNearestLooks` wraps yaw to [0, 360), which is needed to match vanilla at the 13 exact-tie looks of yaw 315.
  - `Blocks.TorchProbe` checks all 4,056 look orders and all 64,896 entries through `FFoundationAuthority::CheckPlacement`, with 0 mismatches, in 0.25 s.
- **Changed cases against T2** (34,632 of 64,896 looks; `C:\dev\sandbox\t3-torch\placement-changes.txt` lists every config):

### Survival, drop and pick (`03a69d1`)

- **Survival:** `FFoundationBlockType::bTorchRule` is set when the registry builds, for the standing block of every `ItemWallBlock` pair and for its wall block. Today that is torch and wall_torch only.
  - `FFoundationAuthority::CanPlantSurvive` checks torch-rule blocks with `FoundationBlockPlacement::IsTorchSupported` (the support table).
  - A torch therefore pops through S7's one-level cascade: the same `Popped` event, no drop (S7's Q4), and the same `Options.bPlantRules` switch.
  - An unknown support cell keeps the torch.
- **Drop and pick:**
  - A wall block without a drop takes its standing block's drop, so a broken wall torch returns a `torch`.
  - `AddBlockItems` skips torch-rule blocks without an item, so no duplicate item appears.
  - `FFoundationInteraction::Pick` also matches `PlacesWallBlock`, so picking a wall torch selects the torch.
- **Tests:** `Blocks.TorchSurvival`, plus interaction scenario `10c. Torches` (a wall torch hung on a wall pops when the wall is mined; exact cell and instance counts).

### The light pool (commit `95ea707`)

- **`VoxelLightSources`** (plain C++):
  - **Light table:** per state, block light at least `voxel.LightMinLevel` (10), never lava. At 10: 1 floor torch state, 4 wall torch states, 17 cube states (glowstone, sea lantern, shroomlight, the three froglights, jack o'lantern, crying obsidian) and 29 model states (cave vines with berries, sea pickles of three or more).
  - **Light points:**
    - floor torch (0.5, 0.5, 0.6875), 1 pixel above the stick;
    - wall torch 0.263 blocks out from the wall and 0.854 up, past the leaning stick's top;
    - a cube 0.6 blocks from its centre along each axis with one open face (a buried cube gets none);
    - other models at the cell centre.
  - **Column scan:** it skips sections whose palette holds no light, through `FVoxelSection::GetPalette()`, a new const accessor. It costs 0.14 to 0.32 µs for a column without emitters and about 25 µs with 9 sources.
  - **Pick:** the nearest K (64, at most 100) within R (48 blocks). Sources strictly under 2 blocks from a pick's seed join it at the intensity-weighted centroid. The pick is deterministic for any input order.
- **`VoxelLights`** (`FVoxelLights` on `AVoxelWorld`):
  - **Lights:** movable `UPointLightComponent`s that cast shadows and allow MegaLights, the settings of T1's megalights mode.
    - attenuation radius (level - 1) blocks, source radius 5 cm, inverse-squared falloff;
    - intensity `voxel.LightIntensity` (1,000 cd) times the pick's weight (level / 14);
    - colour temperature `voxel.LightTemperature` (3,000 K).
  - **Live only while `r.MegaLights.EnableForProject` is 1 and `voxel.Lights` is on.** Otherwise every component is destroyed and none is created, so the default game is unchanged.
  - **When it updates:** it re-picks when a column in reach changes its sources, when the camera moves a block, or when a setting changes; never per frame. Components follow picks by key, and freed ones stay hidden for reuse while live.
  - **Settings:** the cvars are file-local. `voxel.LightsInfo` reports the pool.
  - **Rebasing** leaves the components to the engine's shift.
- **Hooks:**
  - The column mesh job gathers its column's sources on the worker, even while the pool is off, so switching it on needs no remesh.
  - `UVoxelWorldSubsystem` hands them over on apply, clears them on release and resets on stop.
  - A torch edit reaches the pool through its column's remesh.
- **Tests:** `Voxel.Lights` (66 rows, including a brute-force scan comparison) and `Voxel.LightPool` (36 rows; the real cvars are toggled and restored with their set-by priority). `TestVoxel.ps1` expects 103.

## 5. Known issues

- **The default look** (MegaLights off, glow 14) still shows the white torch until Jesus picks.
- **Glow lichen** reads near-white for the same reason; untouched, it needs its own look call.
- **Lit full cubes** (furnace, copper bulb, redstone lamp and ore) carry no per-state light: `DA_Blocks` imports it only for model blocks. They neither glow when lit nor get a light. When: the next blocks-import session.
- **Torch rule for other pairs:** every item with `PlacesWallBlock`, and every `ItemWallBlock` pair, goes through the torch rule. When: before the coral wall fans, signs, banners or heads are wired.
- **Lava and water:** lava should destroy a torch and flowing water should drop it. When: fluids flow.
- **`GetNearestLooks`** now wraps yaw to [0, 360). It differs from before only at exact negative tie yaws, which a continuous aim never hits.
- **The Playground** has no torch lights. **Merged lights** move slightly when a group's seed changes. **Columns keep their sources** while the pool is off (memory only).
- **The capture exposure** sits on the project's auto-exposure floor in every dark view, so the rooms never adapt. The project's floor is EV100 6, the auto-exposure minimum T1 recorded (scout N reads the same at `41190e9`). The "EV 7" in T3's agent reports and run notes is not an EV100: it is log2 of the exposure scale read back at that floor, 0.0078125 = 2^-7.
- **Not built in T3 (Jesus):** particles, a held light, flame-only emission.
