Lane `feature/survival-blocks`, October 15, 2026. The first part of [the blocks plan](2026-10-14-Blocks-Plan.md)'s B3 row after [B2b](2026-10-15-Blocks-B2b.md): doors (two halves, hinge, both halves open), trapdoors (facing and half from the click), fence gates (open, re-face away from the player), iron doors and trapdoors without hand use, door survival. ## 1. What changed

- **`FoundationBlockFamilies.h/.cpp`:** `Door` and `Trapdoor` join `EFoundationBlockFamily` (`Block.Doors`, `Block.Trapdoors`); `foundation.Blocks.Interact` (default 1); `UsesFamilyPlacement`, `IsUsable`, `Use`, `GetUseSound`, `GetSurvival` (`EFoundationFamilySurvival`). Door placement: facing the look, the hinge as the probe measured, the lower half (the authority writes the upper half through the plant rules' pairing). Trapdoor placement: the clicked side face with the slab rule's half, else opposite the look. The use flips `open` (both door halves in one write); an opening gate that faces the player turns to the look.
- **Hinge** (`GetDoorHingeSide`), measured by the new headless probe `Tests/Probes/DoorHingeProbe.java` (11,100 `useOn` placements, `door-hinge-26.3.json`): count the full-block-collision cells per side (lower and upper); right with a door's lower half on the left only, or more full cells on the right; else left with a door's lower half on the right only, or more on the left; else the clicked half, left on the centre line. Not symmetric: a left door beats the counts, a right door does not (parts D and E were added after the review asked about the conflict and the centre).
- **Authority (call lines):** `FindBlockUse` falls back to the family use (iron has none: the click passes and the preview shows the placement, Codex 12 (k)); `CommitBlockUse` (public, Codex 12 (g)) writes a use's states, settles each cell through `SettlePlants`, adds the new `BlockUsed` event (appended last); door survival in `CheckPlantPlacement` and `CanPlantSurvive`; `SettlePlants`' `PopOtherHalf` keeps a partner the cell still pairs with (a toggled door; tall grass replaced by tall grass now pops nothing, the world is the same); `SettlePlants` rings every changed cell (B2b known issue 1).
- **View and controller:** `BlockUsed` plays its event at volume 1, pitch 0.9 + 0.1U (R5); the controller's event log names it `used`.
- **Per-state light:** `BlockImportData.py` emits `state_light` for the 15 full-cube blocks whose states' light differs from the block's (furnace, smoker, blast_furnace, redstone_ore, deepslate_redstone_ore, redstone_lamp, respawn_anchor, the 8 copper bulbs); `ImportBlocks.py` writes it; `VoxelBlocks.cpp` reads `GetLightEmission(State)` for every state (it read the block's light for full cubes). `FoundationBlockRegistry.cpp` already loaded `StateLight` for every block. Lit furnaces glow 13, lit redstone ore 9 (moved pin: `VoxelLightTests` "lit redstone_ore gets no light" now asserts 9 and no light source, below `voxel.LightMinLevel` 10; source `block_state_facts.json`).
- **Reference (Codex 16 #1):** `tools/verify_reference.py` expands `"m"` runs as it expands `"r"` (`expand_runs`, `covers_once`) and self-tests a synthetic non-empty `m` (one pass, two fail cases). `build_reference.py --sums-only` rewrote `SHA256SUMS.txt` (one line). No data file changed; `frozen_models.txt` holds.
- **`DA_Blocks`:** re-imported once for both (assets moved to `C:\dev\sandbox\b3a-blocks\import\assets-pre-b3a`; the three texture arrays restored byte-identical; a second import is a no-op). T3D: 16 of 1,114,342 lines differ, `SourceSha256` and the 15 blocks' lines, which differ only by `StateLight`.
- **Docs:** `Blocks.md` "Doors, trapdoors and gates (B3a)", the light line, a placement row, the tests, the saved-door gap; `Breaking.md` the switch row; `Tests/Probes/README.md` the probe.

## 3. Known issues

1. **When:** worlds saved before B3a. A door stored as one lower half stays one half (no migration); a use opens it alone.
2. **When:** a door's other half sits in a cell nobody knows (the streamed world's edge). The use is consumed and refused (`OutsideBounds`); nothing changes.
3. **When:** placing a door beside glass panes, slabs or other partial blocks. Only full-block collision counts for the hinge (measured for stone, glass, leaves, a bottom slab and a fence); other shapes follow the same box test, not a measurement.
4. **When:** reading the log of a door use's events. `CommitBlockUse` settles each written cell, so a door's neighbour ring runs once per half (harmless, both read the final states).
5. **Any RunChecks run (flake):** the voxel step crashed once in `VoxelWorldgen::ReadFeatures` (an access violation in the parallel decoration read) and passed on the re-run; re-run before suspecting code, and report it if it repeats.
6. B2b's issues 2 and 3 (unknown cells read as air; old family blocks keep stored states) stand.
