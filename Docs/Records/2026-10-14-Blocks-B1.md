# Blocks B1: the reference extension (survival track T5)

Lane `feature/survival-blocks`, October 14, 2026. [The blocks plan](2026-10-14-Blocks-Plan.md) section 2. B1 adds 310 vanilla 26.3 blocks as data: the 302 family blocks (stairs, fences, fence gates, walls, panes and bars, doors, trapdoors, buttons, lever, pressure plates, ladder, nether vines, beds, chests, farmland and dirt path, crops, saplings) and the PM's 8 (cauldron, water, lava and powder-snow cauldrons, fire, soul fire, campfire, soul campfire). Each places its default state. Shapes, connections, use, climbing, farming and beds are B2-B7.

The creative screen is cached, so set the switch at launch.
- **Blocks reference** (`Reference/minecraft-blocks-26.3`, rebuilt through its tools): 926 blocks (395 model blocks), 28,552 states, 965 textures (966 of 1,024 codes, 58 left), 1,364 of 2,048 layers, 4,867 baked models (most from stair shapes), 887 items (+286), new `facts/family_survival.json` and `facts/block_use_sounds.csv`, new `stand_ins/`.
  - `build_manifest.py`: 18 model groups after the torch; `added: "survival-B1"` on the new entries; `stand_in: true` on the chests.
  - `build_reference.py`: `APPENDED_BLOCKS` (torch, wall torch, then the 310 in family order); `ITEMS_REFERENCE_ITEMS` (the 9 saplings' items live in the items reference); every texture already present keeps its code (the cauldrons reuse `water_still` and `lava_still`); the chest stand-in box `[1,0,1]-[15,14,15]` with 18 tiles cropped and flipped from `textures/entity/chest/{normal,trapped,ender}.png`; the `stem_age` tint; composite item models (the 16 bed icons: head plus foot translated one block south); `bamboo_fence(_gate)_particle` handled like the shelf mushroom's.
  - `bake_models.py`: uvlock for partial faces; `bake_composite`.
  - `FactsDump.java`: a third argument, `tools/survival_base_ids.txt` (the 618 frozen subjects, so `plant_survival.json` stays byte-identical); `dumpFamilySurvival` (subjects are states that fail alone or with stone at one neighbour, which brings in farmland and dirt path; candidates are air plus every state; 3,472 subjects, about 110 s); `dumpUseSounds` (doors by the public `DoorBlock.type()`; trapdoors, gates, buttons and plates by matching the id to the public `BlockSetType`/`WoodType` values, checked by sound type).
