## 2. Decisions

- **The grid lives on the inventory** (`Craft[4]`, next to `Carried`): the authority already snapshots and revisions it. The result is computed, not stored, so the switch and a rules rebuild can't leave it stale.
- **A new asset, not DA_Items** (the manager's answer 1); `/Game/Items` is already cooked, and no script there deletes unknown assets.
- **The full jar recipe set goes in the reference;** `BuildRules` drops what today's items can't make, so new items bring their recipes with them.
- **Shift-craft crafts only while the whole result fits** (vanilla's partial case is not documented; conservative).
- **Throws stay T2's:** Q on the result and a close whose grid doesn't fit go through the existing `DropDeferred` refusals.
- Vanilla plays no crafting sound; none is played.
