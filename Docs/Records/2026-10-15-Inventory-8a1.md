## What it does

- `foundation.Furnace 1` (default): a furnace keeps its slots in a block-entity store keyed by world cell (`Foundation/FoundationContainers.*`), and every furnace in a loaded column burns fuel and smelts each step, open or not. 0: a furnace opens and holds items but never ticks.
- Right-click opens the furnace's menu (`EFoundationMenuKind::Furnace`: 0 input, 1 fuel, 2 result, 3-29 main, 30-38 hotbar). **No screen in 8a1** (SM Q1): the panels refuse it and the pawn closes the menu at once; the screen is 8a2's.
- Cooking data comes from the 26.3 jar's cooking recipes plus the value report's fuel table, generated by `Scripts/GenerateCookingData.py` into `Foundation/FoundationCookingData.inl`. It is checked against `survival.r3.recipes` (116 recipes) and `survival.r3.fuels` (347 fuels), outside `Reference/`.
- Taking any of the output pays the stored XP as orbs (SM Q2: in 8a1, through `SpawnExperience`). Breaking a furnace drops its slots, pays its stored XP and closes its menu.
- Furnace contents last for the session (PM 3): no save file, no version bump. They are saved with the other block edits at the voxel plan's phase 10.
