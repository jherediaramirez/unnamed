## Behavior

`voxel.Far.Trees 1` retains actual above-ground wood and every leaf tier in compact vertical runs. Matching exposed faces merge; state/axis, tint, snow tops and real air gaps remain. Each column emits only its own cells. `voxel.Far.Trees 0` (evaluation default) restores the old upper-canopy-only path. Cutout leaves remain the recommended look (`voxel.Far.CanopyLayer 0`); existing materials, fog and lighting are unchanged.

The solid-shape guard matters: `_stem` also matches crop models. Only full-cube log/wood/stem/hyphae states enter the new wood runs. Crop stems stay omitted models rather than becoming wood boxes; the legacy class table is unchanged.

At column boundaries, tree pieces retain conservative caps where an adjacent column may still show legacy geometry. Neighbor tree runs are not built: the border policy uses their existing ground and upper-canopy summary only. Extra opposing faces can remain hidden inside a tree, but no whole tree is instantiated twice. Across near overhangs the actual border's open cells take precedence over the heightfield. The switch converges per column, not atomically across the forest.

Far-option revisions purge obsolete queued results and reject old-option in-flight results, including 0 -> 1 -> 0. A purge resets `MeshedKind` as required by A1. Already displayed geometry stays until replacement. Ordinary edit-stale acceptance and apply scheduling are unchanged.
