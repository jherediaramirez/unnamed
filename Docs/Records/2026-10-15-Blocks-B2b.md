# Blocks B2b: placement shapes and the neighbour pass (survival track T5)

Lane `feature/survival-blocks`, October 15, 2026. The rest of [the blocks plan](2026-10-14-Blocks-Plan.md)'s B2 row after [B2a](2026-10-15-Blocks-B2a.md): stairs (facing, half, shape), fence, wall, pane and bars connections, gate facing and `in_wall`, placed and kept up to date by a one-ring neighbour pass. No reference change. Next: [the B3a handoff](2026-10-15-Blocks-Handoff-B3a.md).

## 1. What changed

- **`FoundationBlockFamilies.h/.cpp` (new):** the family lookup `GetFamily` (`EFoundationBlockFamily`: stairs, fence, wall, pane/bars, fence gate; from the block tags plus the pane/bars property set), `GetSturdyFaces` and `IsFaceSturdy` (collision boxes clipped to the cell cover the face; leaves and powder snow none), `UpdateShape` (one `BlockState.updateShape`), `SettleShape` (the oracle's settle rule), `GetStateForPlacement` and `UpdateNeighbourShapes`. All under `foundation.Blocks.Shapes` (B2a's switch, default 1); 0 is B1's placement and no pass.
- **Placement:** `FoundationBlockPlacement::GetStateForPlacement` hands family blocks to the families with the slab rule's half. Stairs and gates face the player's horizontal look (gates: VALUES "Fence gates", `blocks.fence_gate_placement`); `waterlogged` in a water source; then the shape settles from the six neighbours.
- **Neighbour pass:** one line at the top of `FFoundationAuthority::SettlePlants` (every edit path goes through it, and it runs with `bPlantRules` off too): the 4 horizontal neighbours of the edited cell, and a wall below it, take one `updateShape` toward it. One ring, no cascade.
- **Exceptions measured by the oracle** (`single_neighbour`; each would fail it without the rule): leaves (`#minecraft:leaves`) and `powder_snow` have no sturdy face; `pumpkin`, `carved_pumpkin`, `jack_o_lantern` and `melon` never connect.
- **Wall rules:** a side is tall when the block above's underside covers a 2-pixel strip from that edge past the centre; the post follows minecraft.wiki "Wall" (override tag or a wall with its post above; an end, corner or lone post; else only a covered centre while no two opposite sides are tall). The 32 `wall_up` rows and the single and cross-family rows agree; the strips are the only shape that fits them that we tried, not a measured constant.
- **Voxel gather (B2a known issue 2):** `AVoxelWorld::GatherCollision`'s column loop moved unchanged into the static `AVoxelWorld::GatherColumns` (only `->` to `.` and `Authority.World` to `World`; the reviewer confirmed it identical), so `Unnamed.Voxel.World.ShapesGather` runs it on a streamed column without a world.
- **Docs:** `Blocks.md` "Shapes and connections (B2b)", a placement row and the tests; `Breaking.md`'s switch row.

## 3. Known issues

1. **When:** a cell `SettlePlants` pops or writes after the edit (a popped plant, a written upper half) sits beside a family block. The pass rings only the edited cell. No family block reads such a cell today; B3's door halves will (one more call per changed cell).
2. **When:** a rule reads a cell nobody knows (the edge of the streamed world). It reads air, so a wall there shows low or no side until the cell loads and is edited.
3. **When:** worlds saved before B2b. Their family blocks keep their stored (default) states until a neighbour changes; nothing re-settles on load.
4. **When:** reading `GetFamily` for B3. Doors, trapdoors and buttons are not in it yet; B3 adds them (`#minecraft:doors`, `trapdoors`, `buttons`).
5. **When:** reading a log of wall states: one mutation log printed an unset wall side as `None` (FName `none` is `NAME_None`). Cosmetic; the code compares through the `NoSide` constant.
6. B2a's issues 3-5 (uvlock, the oracle's format wording, carpets in `wall_up`) stand.
