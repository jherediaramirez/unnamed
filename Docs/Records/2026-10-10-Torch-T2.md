# Torch T2: torch data (reference, support table, item)

Torch session T2, October 10, 2026, parallel lane `feature/voxel-light` (from `a7e1bf3`). Night manager Nyx confirmed the plan and its changes; the handoff was [2026-10-10-Torch-Handoff-T2.md](2026-10-10-Torch-Handoff-T2.md).

## What changed

- **Blocks reference:** `torch` and `wall_torch` are in `Reference/minecraft-blocks-26.3`, regenerated through its own tools. The reference now has 616 blocks (85 model blocks), 4,424 states, 308 baked models, 690 textures and 601 block items.
  - `tools/build_manifest.py`: a `torch` model group.
  - `tools/build_reference.py`: `APPENDED_BLOCKS = ["torch", "wall_torch"]`. Their new textures and models go after every earlier one: torch is texture index 690 (layer 944), and its models are 303-307. Blocks, states, items and facts stay in registry order. The states loop became `bake(blocks)` and the block-and-item loop `merge(blocks)`, run in the order base, base item icons, appended.
  - `tools/verify_reference.py`: the new counts, the append invariant (the textures and models only appended blocks use must be the last ones; a mutation test with `dead_bush` prepended fails it), and the support table checks.
  - `tools/FactsDump.java`: `dumpSupport` writes `facts/block_support.json`, described below.
- **Support table:** one face mask per imported state, from the torch's own `canSurvive`. The world holds only the supporting state: a `java.lang.reflect.Proxy` of the public `LevelReader`, air elsewhere, and any unhandled method fails the dump.
  - Up: a floor torch on top. A side: a wall torch against it, facing away. Down: never.
  - Results: 2,636 states hold all five, 248 hold the top only, and 1,540 hold none.
    - Leaves and powder snow hold none.
    - An upside-down slab holds a floor torch.
    - A bottom slab holds nothing. That is vanilla's answer to scout G's open probe P1.
    - Snow layers 1-7 hold nothing, and 8 holds all five.
    - Some model states hold a torch: azalea, mangrove roots, the amethyst buds, pointed dripstone, sulfur spike.
  - `items`: the standing-and-wall pairs from the public `BlockItem.registerBlocks`: the torch, and the five coral fans.
- **Import and runtime:**
  - `DA_Blocks`: `StateSupport` per state, `ItemWallBlock` (torch: `wall_torch`), and `EFoundationPlacementRule::WallAttached` for the wall torch.
  - `FFoundationBlockRegistry::GetTorchSupport`. Project blocks' full cubes hold top and sides.
  - `FFoundationItemDefinition::PlacesWallBlock`.
  - `FoundationBlockPlacement::GetItemBlock` and `HasTorchSupport`. `CheckPlacement` places the wall torch on a side face, the floor torch on a top face, and refuses an underside click (`CannotSurvive`) until T3.
- **Item:** the `torch` block item (stack 64, flat sprite of `block/torch`, first in Functional Blocks). `wall_torch` has none (S5's rule).
  - The coral fans' items do not get their wall fans yet: the importer takes wall blocks only for the attachment the table measures.
- **Item reference cascade, Nyx's answer (a):**
  - The item reference hashes the blocks reference's `block_manifest.json` as an input. Its own `build_reference.py`, run in a sandbox, changed only `manifest.json` (that input hash), `facts/break_times.json` (torch and wall torch `imported` false to true) and two `SHA256SUMS.txt` lines. Those three files were copied in.
  - `DA_Items` was re-imported by the documented route. Its T3D export is identical apart from `SourceSha256`, and the 70 item sprites are unchanged.

## Gaps and known issues

- **Underside clicks** place nothing. Vanilla tries the item's other faces by look direction (probe P3); T3 brings it with the support table.
- **No survival yet:** a torch whose support is mined stays (T3, after S7's cascade). Mining a wall torch drops nothing: it has no item, and the provisional drop rule drops a block's own item.
- **Replaceable clicks:** clicking the side of a replaceable block (short grass) puts the wall torch in that cell and checks the cell behind its facing, not the clicked block. That matches vanilla's use of the state's facing, but T3 should confirm it against vanilla.
- **Coral fans** place only their floor fan. Their wall fans need their own attachment probe.
- **`creative_tabs.json` is not reproducible byte for byte:** FactsDump prints Java identity hashes in component patches. A fix would change that file's format, so it is left for whoever next needs the tabs re-dumped.
- **`Docs/VoxelWorld.md`** still says the voxel table copies "303 baked models" (S6's text); it is 308 now. Left to the main lane, which owns that doc tonight.
- The Playground draws no model blocks in this lane (S6b merges separately), and the voxel world would draw a placed torch with its whole model emitting at 14 (S6's rule). Light is T3.
