## 1. What changed

- **Pop drops (Jesus's play bug):** a block that pops (`SettlePlants`' `Pop`: a stack above a broken segment, a plant,
  torch or door half whose support went) or is washed away (`SettleAfterFluid`) drops what an empty hand breaking that
  block alone drops: `RollBlockDrops(..., Rules.Hand, MakeSeed(LootSeed, cell, Pops))` before the block goes,
  `SpawnDrops` after, only with `Options.bItemEntities`. A 3-tall sugar cane drops 3. A two-half block drops once:
  with a 26.3 table both halves roll and its `half=lower` condition picks one (sunflower); without one (doors, "drops
  itself") the other half does not roll. Both sites share `FindAuthorityLootTable` (file-local, FoundationAuthority.cpp).
- **Firefly bush removed (beyond vanilla; Jesus via PM 4: "Stop generating, hide it", then "Short grass"):**
  `FVoxelDecorationOptions::bFireflyBushAsShortGrass` swaps `firefly_bush` for `short_grass` in the compiled
  providers and `would_survive` tests (`FVoxelStateProviders::ReplaceState`, `FVoxelBlockPredicates::ReplaceState`; 3
  swapped), so its three patches (`patch_firefly_bush_near_water`, `_near_water_swamp`, `_swamp`) place short grass
  with the same tries, offsets and predicates. Switch `voxel.Decoration.FireflyBushAsGrass` (default 1; 0 restores
  the bush), read by streamed worlds (a change rebuilds the world), `voxel.Bench`, the model bench and biome debug.
  `Mesher.Digest` keeps 26.3's decoration (it guards the mesher, not worldgen). The creative tabs and search hide it
  (`HudCreative.cpp`, `IsHiddenCreativeItem`). Block, item, saved ids and the `DA_Blocks` entry are unchanged. The
  overlay-lift fix for its black look was dropped with it (the bush still draws black where placed by hand).
- **Chest no-mesh deferred with the lid (feature freeze):** built, then dropped by Jesus's freeze ("Core lines +
  day/night", via PM 4) before landing: chests keep drawing their block mesh until the lid animation lands after QC.
  The importer flag, the mesh skips and their tests were removed; `DA_Blocks` is byte-identical to before B4a
  (ImportBlocks verify PASS; T3D diff 0 lines, `C:\dev\sandbox\b4a-blocks\import\t3d-freeze-summary.txt`). What the
  build showed, for the lid step: outline and collision come from the registry's box table, not the quads
  (`FoundationBlockRegistry.cpp` `GetOutlineBoxes`/`GetModelBounds`), and the Playground probe's
  `CountExpectedBlockInstances` must learn any no-mesh rule.
