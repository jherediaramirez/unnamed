# Survival 20b2a: chunk-generation packs, mob hazards and the Codex 19 folds (record)

Lane `feature/survival-mobs` (base 4625b579, 20b1 merged with `feature/survival` dcd2bcee), survival track T6, run 2.
20b2 was split (SM, 2026-10-15): this is 20b2a; the 400-tick cycle and the caps are 20b2b
([the 20b2b handoff](2026-10-15-Mobs-Handoff-20b2b.md)). Switches: `foundation.Mobs` as before, new
`foundation.Mobs.Spawn` (1 default; 0 = mobs only from `foundation.Mobs.Summon`).

## 1. What was built

- **Biome spawn table** (`DA_Mobs.BiomeSpawns`, `WorldgenSha256`): `MobImportData.biome_spawns()` reads every biome's
  `natural_mob_spawns.creature` (type, weight, `count` or a uniform's bounds) and
  `creature_world_gen_spawn_probability` (0.1 without it) from `Reference/minecraft-worldgen-26.3` (hash-checked
  against its `SHA256SUMS.txt`, only read). Every entity is kept; `UFoundationMobAssets::AddSpawns` keeps the built,
  spawnable ones in `Rules.BiomeSpawns` (O9: plains sheep 12, pig 10, chicken 10, cow 8). Import:
  `MOB_IMPORT PASS: ... worldgen 0de8eb4679ec, 56 biome spawn lists (171 creature entries); M_Mob overlay multiply`.
- **Chunk-generation packs** (`FFoundationAuthority::StepMobSpawns`, `SpawnPack`, `FindPackCell`): each step, the
  columns (16 x 16) within 10 of a live player that are loaded and not yet seen roll once: a random column of the chunk,
  the biome above its top block (`GetBiomeId`, set by `AVoxelWorld` from the column's biome and the terrain's biome
  table), the biome's chance, a species by weight (`PickSpawn`), a size from the entry (`RollPackSize`), and each
  member offset by `RandRange(0,5) - RandRange(0,5)` per axis, kept inside the chunk, standing on the top
  motion-blocking block, which must be a grass block, with room for its box and no fluid (light proxy 15 >= 9).
  Deterministic from the terrain's seed (`MobSpawnSeed`) and the column. The Playground has no biome hook: no spawning.
- **Hazards** (Codex 19 #1, `StepMobHazards`): `FFoundationVitals::StepHazards` is `Step`'s fall and fire block, now
  shared (the player's `Step` calls it; Vitals suites green); mobs land with the player's fall damage (the chicken
  immune), take lava (4, 300 fire ticks) and burning (1 every 20) with `foundation.Fire` (`Options.bFire`, copied by
  the pawn), and water puts the fire out (`IsMobInFluid`, the generated world's fluid-box test). A hazard hit shows the
  overlay and plays hurt/death; a burning death rolls the loot with `is_on_fire` (cooked meat).
- **Death drops** (Codex 19 #5, settled by R20 `survival2.r20.mob_death`): loot and experience drop on the killing hit
  (`Attack`, `StepMobHazards`); the corpse goes at death tick 20.
- **Folds**: knockback `vy = min(0.4, vy/2 + power)` (#3, V:512, R20 mob_physics); the view fills its 128 slots per type
  nearest first (#2); steps play at pitch 1 (#4, F 3582), ambient/hurt/death at the random pitch; the A* budget rotates
  (`PathStart`: after a step where mobs waited, the next starts after the last one planned; the rest plan after the
  loop); `M_Mob`'s overlay multiplies the skin by (1, 0.55, 0.55) (`ImportMobs.py` `OVERLAY_MODE = "multiply"`; `"mix"`
  is the 0.3 mix toward red).

## 2. Decisions

- Split 20b2 into 20b2a / 20b2b (SM, Q1). `FoundationVitals` refactor and the two pawn lines approved (SM, Q2).
- Drops on the killing hit (SM, R20). Gen chances are data, not provisional (SM).
- Light: the settled proxy (sky-exposed above the motion-blocking top = 15), so leaves block spawning beneath them.
- Packs keep every reference entity in `DA_Mobs`; the game filters, so a mob built later needs no reimport.
- Step pitch is decided in the view from the event name (`.step`), so `FFoundationEvent` (hot header) is unchanged.

## 4. Known issues, with when

- The 400-tick cycle, caps, rounds, distances: 20b2b.
- Packs are rolled once per column per session; a reopened world rolls again (mobs are not saved): with mob saving.
- Hazards: no panic and no recent-attacker memory (a hazard death after a player's hit pays no experience): 20c
  (hostiles need the attacker memory too).
- Grazing particles and sound: silent hooks (unchanged).
- Review leftovers: no cap on chunk-generation packs (vanilla ignores the cap there too; the view draws the nearest
  128 per type); pack mobs' ids (their AI and loot streams) depend on column load order; pack members clamped to the
  chunk can pile on its edge cells and do not check other mobs; the seen-set never shrinks. 20b2b or later.
- No runtime evidence of spawned animals in a generated world yet: 20b2b's first task (a count line and a shot).
- Baby models have their own geometry (R17/R20 `baby_sizes`, not adult x 0.5): 20e.
