## 1. What changed

- **Switch** `foundation.Blocks.Shapes` (`FoundationBlockRegistry.cpp`, `FoundationBlockFamilies::AreShapesEnabled`, default 1). 0 is B1's behaviour. B2b puts placement and the neighbour pass under it too.
- **Collision gather:** `FFoundationBlockWorld::GatherCollision` and `AVoxelWorld::GatherCollision` (model states) also read the cell layers just below and just above a region. From those layers they keep only boxes that leave their own cell vertically and overlap the region with volume (`FoundationCollisionGather::KeepLeaving`, `FoundationBlocks.h/.cpp`). Fences, walls and closed gates now collide 1.5 blocks tall, and the pitcher crop's lower half reaches 1/16 below its cell (it pokes down, so "one cell lower" alone, the plan's wording, would have missed it). With the switch at 0 the gather is B1's box list exactly, in order. The voxel gather no longer returns early when only the outer layers reach the world's height range (review fix).
- **Oracle:** `Reference/minecraft-blocks-26.3/facts/shape_updates.json` (2.56 MB): `BlockState.updateShape` answers measured by `FactsDump.java` (`dumpShapeUpdates`) through its public `LevelReader` proxy, for 182 subjects (97 stairs, 14 fences, 32 walls, 13 gates, 26 panes and bars). The sections:
  - single neighbours (7 representatives × 6 directions × air and all 28,552 states);
  - a cross-family sample (195 neighbour states);
  - walls' up and tall rows;
  - stair shapes (single and two-neighbour cases);
  - gates in walls.

  The settle rule: update toward down, up, north, south, west, east, until stable, at most 3 passes. Every case settled in one pass. Its bottom-stair corners are the 32 cases of VALUES.md "Stair corners" (evidence `blocks.stairs`, `blocks.stair_corners`): 16 outer, 16 inner. The reference README has a "Shape updates" section.
- **Model order frozen (Codex 15 #3):**
  - `build_reference.py` bakes and merges appended blocks in batches (`APPENDED_BATCH_STARTS`, today one batch: torch + B1), each batch's item icons before the next batch.
  - `tools/frozen_models.txt` lists the sha256 (`model_digest`) of the 4,867 accepted baked models. The build and `verify_reference.py` require them unchanged at their indices.
  - The build refuses a missing or short freeze file and duplicate batch starts.
- **Hidden saplings pass (Codex 15 #1):** `FoundationAuthority.cpp` `UseOn` step 3: a hidden family block (`foundation.Blocks.Families 0`) counts as no place capability, so the use passes to the offhand, as before B1.
- **Panes translucent (Codex 15 #2):** `VoxelBlocks.cpp` gives a translucent block's model states (the 18 panes, the water cauldron) `EVoxelLayer::Translucent`; `VoxelMesher.cpp` `Model()` writes into that section. `M_ChunkTranslucent` shares `CHUNK_BLOCK_HLSL` with the cutout material, so model quads draw the same. Generated worlds hold no translucent model state.
- **Saplings drop themselves (Codex 15 #4, confirmed: all 9 dropped nothing):** `FFoundationBlockRegistry::Build` takes DA_Items (`GetDefault` passes `UFoundationItemAssets::LoadDefault()`): a block with no item of its own that a DA_Items item places drops that item (`bDropsPlacingItem`; `AddBlockItems` then makes no duplicate block item). Only the 9 saplings match. B8's loot tables replace it. No DA_Items or items-reference change.
- **Stem tint (Codex 15 #5):** not changed; B1 known issue 2, owned by B6 with a per-age tint test.
- **Reference:** rebuilt through its tool (research `C:\dev\sandbox\b1-blocks\research`, facts `C:\dev\sandbox\b2-blocks\ref\buildin\facts`). The README's rebuild section now names the research folder that builds it. `DA_Blocks` and its three texture arrays were re-imported for the new `SHA256SUMS.txt` digest.
- **Docs:** `Blocks.md` (the uvlock known issue narrowed; "Shapes and collision (B2a)"; the sapling loot line), `Breaking.md` (the switch row).

## 3. Known issues

1. **When:** now; **fix:** B2b. Nothing connects; stairs are straight; every family block places its default state.
2. **When:** any voxel-world collision near a fence. `AVoxelWorld::GatherCollision`'s outer layers have no native test of their own (they share `KeepLeaving` with the tested block-world path; the flight and probe exercise the voxel path). B2b can add one with a generated column.
3. **When:** viewing fence-bar or open-gate undersides, or a pressed wall button. The uvlock order for partial faces is measured but not changed ([Blocks](../Blocks.md), known gaps); B3 checks the buttons.
4. **When:** reading `shape_updates.json`'s format. "One pass" means no second changing pass; the wording says "more than one pass" (pre-commit review, low; changing it changes the file).
5. **When:** a later batch imports carpets. `wall_up` then gains rows (FactsDump adds `white_carpet` above walls only if imported); the verifier pins 32 rows and will fail, as intended, until the pin is moved.
6. The water cauldron now draws in the voxel world's translucent section (B1 known issue 3: one translucent block, accepted).
7. Mining a sapling drops it through the registry's self-drop, not a loot table (no explosion or silk-touch rules; saplings have none in vanilla either).
