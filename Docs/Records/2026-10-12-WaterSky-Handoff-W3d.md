## Plan notes (from W3c's approved plan)

- **Opacity and streaks:** new `MPC_VoxelLook` scalars (for example `WaterOpacity`, `WaterStreaks`) as `voxel.Look.*` dials, with defaults that first keep today's look. Then every control pair is one switch, with no asset swapping.
  - Add them to `LOOK_PARAMETERS`. `look_collection()` appends new parameters to the existing collection and keeps old ids.
  - Regenerate only `M_BlockWater`, `M_ChunkWater` and `M_ChunkFarWater` (`-Materials=`), and hash `Content/` before and after.
  - Show the manager a ladder with control pairs and the vanilla measure:
    - coherence 0.5-0.7 on vanilla's cells;
    - the floor's show-through at 1, 2 and 4 deep (the pool scene gives 1; the probe adds more).
  - A value measured closer to vanilla and one switch away is the manager's call (flagged to Jesus); anything else goes to Jesus with captures.
- **Water fog** (this session's, apart from DN2's edge fog):
  - **Material:** a post-process material `M_WaterFog` generated by the materials script (not hand-made). It is on only while the camera's eye is in a water cell, in a new `VoxelWaterFog.h/.cpp` with a one-line hook in `AVoxelWorld::Tick` after `UpdateEdgeFog`.
  - **Colour:** each biome's `minecraft:visual/water_fog_color`. Most biomes leave it unset, and the default is `#050533` (the `wiki_default` in `Reference/minecraft-sky-26.3/attributes.json`). The biomes that set one keep it in `Reference/minecraft-worldgen-26.3/data/minecraft/worldgen/biome/*.json`, under the effects (8 distinct values).
  - **Distances:** start -8 and end 96 blocks (`water_fog_start_distance` and `water_fog_end_distance` in the same attributes file, from the wiki). Cite them in the record.
  - **Edge fog:** under water the fog is full by 96 blocks, so it covers the edge fog; above water it is off.
  - **Open item:** vanilla's underwater view growing clearer over time stays open.
  - **Cost:** give the GPU cost under water, fog on against off, as information.
- **Rules from the manager:**
  - numbers come from game data, published docs and black-box runs (`javap -public` signatures are fine; never bytecode bodies);
  - a Sonnet pre-commit review of the diff;
  - `Scripts\RunChecks.ps1 -Profile final -Flight smoke` after the last code change.
- **Overlap:** `CHUNK_WATER_HLSL` also feeds `M_ChunkFarWater`, which far LOD step 3 may touch. Keep the hunks to the water materials, and say so in the plan.
