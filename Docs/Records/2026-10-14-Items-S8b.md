## 1. What was built

- **Throwing** (`FFoundationAuthority::Drop`, the `Drop` command's `bWholeStack`): Q throws one item of the selected main-hand stack and Ctrl+Q the whole stack, in survival and creative. Vanilla's creative Q takes from the stack too, and the manager approved it. The item leaves 0.3 blocks below the eye at 0.3 blocks per tick along the look, plus 0.1 up and a small random spread. Its pickup delay is 40, so the earliest pickup is the 40th step. An empty hand is refused (`NoSupply`), and so is a failed spawn, without touching the stack. With the switch at 0, Drop is refused (`DropDeferred`) as before. `FoundationInteraction.cpp` and the controller already sent the command; they are unchanged.
- **Damage:** `FFoundationItemEntity::Damage`; `SpawnItem(..., Damage = 0)`; `FFoundationInventory::Add(..., bTouchStacks = true, Damage = 0)` merges only into stacks with the same damage and gives new stacks that damage. Every existing caller is unchanged, because durable items stack to 1.
- **Merge** (`FFoundationAuthority::MergeItemEntities`, after the motion loop and before the pickups): every 40 steps of its own age, a live item (the seeker) takes in same-item, same-damage, non-frozen stacks whose box strictly overlaps its box grown 0.5 sideways and 0.25 up and down (`GetMergeBox`), up to the max stack. The smaller stack goes into the larger, into the seeker on a tie. The result keeps the longer delay and the younger age.
- **Despawn:** an item goes when its age reaches 6,000 (in the motion loop, before the pickups). Its age does not count while frozen.
- **Copies** (`FoundationItemEntities::GetCopies`; `FFoundationItemEntityView::SetCopies` and `GetDrawnCopies`): 1 copy, then 2, 3, 4 and 5 from 2, 17, 33 and 49 items. A block item adds instances to its block's instanced components. A sprite item adds static mesh components (`SpriteCopies`). Each copy after the first is offset by up to 0.15 blocks on each axis (half that for sprites), fixed by the entity id.
- **The switch:** the same `foundation.Items.Entities` (`Options.bItemEntities`, copied in by the pawn before each step). At 0, Drop is refused and nothing merges or despawns.
- **Docs:** `Docs/Items.md`, "Item entities": the throw, merge and despawn bullet, the known issues, and the Tests entries.
