Lane `feature/survival-blocks`, October 15, 2026. The rest of [the blocks plan](2026-10-14-Blocks-Plan.md)'s B3 row after [B3a](2026-10-15-Blocks-B3a.md): buttons (pressed, released after 20 or 30 steps), the lever (toggles), their wall, floor and ceiling placement and survival, the R13 cross-check, and Codex 28 #1 first (PM 4). Next: [the B4 handoff](2026-10-15-Blocks-Handoff-B4.md) (climbing: ladders, vines).

## 1. What changed

- **Codex 28 #1 (first):** `CommitBlockUse` settles its cells with `SettlePlants(..., bWriteUpperHalf = false)`; only a placement writes an upper half. Using a lone lower door half (an old save, a fixture) no longer overwrites the block above it.
- **`FoundationBlockFamilies.h/.cpp`:** `Button` (`Block.Buttons`, 15) and `Lever` (id `lever`) join `EFoundationBlockFamily`; `UsesFamilyPlacement`, `IsUsable`, `Use`, `GetUseSound` (`click_on`/`click_off`, `block.lever.click`), `GetSurvival` (the sturdy face it hangs on), `GetButtonTicks` (30 `#minecraft:wooden_buttons`, else 20), `ReleaseButton`. Placement: the first of `GetClickLooks` where it survives; up: ceiling, down: floor (both facing the horizontal look), a side: that wall facing away from it. `IsFaceSturdy`: every snow layer's underside is sturdy (one layer has no collision; `family_survival.json`'s up probe, 128 cases).
- **Authority (hot file; API addition for other lines):** `ScheduleBlockTick(Cell, Expect, Delay, EFoundationBlockTick Kind)`, a general scheduled tick: `Step` counts the list down first, a due tick runs only if the cell still holds `Expect`, a new tick replaces the cell's pending tick of the same kind, `Reset` clears them, nothing is saved. Kinds: `ButtonRelease`. `CommitBlockUse` takes a const player and `Volume`/`Pitch` (defaults 1 and 0).
- **Event:** `FFoundationEvent::Pitch` (new field; 0 leaves the pitch to the view); `BlockUsed` carries its volume in `Amount`. No new event kind.
- **View:** `BlockUsed` plays `Amount` and `Pitch`, or 0.9 + 0.1U when `Pitch` is 0 (doors, trapdoors, gates).
- **Probe:** `Tests/Probes/SwitchPlacementProbe.java` (public `useOn`, `useWithoutItem`, `ServerLevel.tick`): parts A and B, 7,920 placements; part C, 15 press runs. SHA-256 `49780CFD…D9F33E` (README). Runs twice byte-identical, about 30 s.
- **R13 cross-check:** `Scripts/MakeR13Oracles.py` writes `Source/Unnamed/Tests/BlockR13Oracles.json` from `SURVIVAL-RUN2-FINDINGS-26.3.md` R13 (SHA-256 of the input recorded inside). Before building on it: R13's boxes for every door, trapdoor, gate, button and lever state (3,616) equal `block_state_facts.json`'s, in order; offline, the other families differ only in box order, the mangrove propagule's position offset, or are blocks we do not import (wool carpets, copper chests, straw bed, attached stems) (`C:\dev\sandbox\b3b-blocks\r13\r13-shapes-summary.txt`).
- **Docs:** `Blocks.md` "Buttons and levers (B3b)", the placement row, the tests, the uvlock note; `Breaking.md` the switch row; `Tests/Probes/README.md`.
