## 1. What was built

- **Runtime spawn evidence** (`AVoxelWorld`): build.txt and the log get a `Mobs:` line (live mobs by type, columns
  seen, packs, pack-spawned, authority steps, cycles run, cycle-spawned, and the last cycle's eligible columns, counted
  creatures and cap). `CaptureVoxel.ps1 -Mobs` adds a "mobs" shot after the others: the nearest live mob under open air
  (6 cells above its feet and the camera's cell) within 160 blocks, from 5 blocks toward the player and 4 up; the camera
  follows the mob while the shot settles and holds still for the last 10 frames. `-MobsSteps <n>` waits for n authority
  steps first. Default shots, names and counts are unchanged (the shot is opt-in; `FinishShots` is the old tail).
  Result: the biome hook works; dev seed 18 live (sheep 15, cow 2, pig 1; 8 packs over 441 columns), seed `meadow` 53.
- **Grazing** (Codex 23 #2): `IsSheepFood` tests the block tag `Block.EdibleForSheep` (`#minecraft:edible_for_sheep`:
  short_grass, fern, short_dry_grass, tall_dry_grass; already in `Config/Tags/MinecraftBlockTags.ini` from DA_Blocks,
  so no constant list and no lock). A plant in the tag where the sheep stands breaks without drops (its remainder);
  else a grass block below turns to dirt.
- **A\*** (Codex 23 #1, `FoundationMobPath.cpp`): a neighbour height rejected as too high to climb (a bottom slab at
  head height) no longer ends the column scan; it keeps looking down, and `GetStand` checks the headroom from the lower
  feet. A height rejected for lack of jump headroom still ends it, as before.
- **The creature cycle** (`RunCreatureCycle`, `SpawnCycleColumn`; constants in `FoundationMobs.h`): on authority steps
  that are multiples of 400 [survival2.r20 population runs], the eligible columns are the loaded ones within 8 of a live
  player (289 for one) [survival.r10.spawning_rules S 5472]; the cap `floor(10 x eligible / 289)` [S 5472; R20: 289,
  creature cap 10]; nothing when the creatures in the eligible columns are at the cap. Otherwise the columns, shuffled
  (deterministic from the seed and the cycle), each spawn while some player whose 17 x 17 holds them is under its local
  cap of 10: up to 3 group rounds, `ceil(U x 4)` attempts each [S 5472]; each attempt is the round's start column
  offset by `RollPackOffset` per axis, at least 24 and at most 128 blocks from the nearest player [S 5472]; the round's
  species by weight from the biome above the first such place, its size from the entry; `FindPackCell` (grass, light
  proxy, room, no fluid); at most 4 a column [Codex 02 B, cluster 4]. A column's packs can overshoot the cap [R20].
  Counters `MobCycles`, `MobsCycleSpawned`, `MobCycleEligible/Count/Cap`.

## 4. Known issues, with when

- A natural-world shot of cycle-spawned animals: needs a sparse spot or animals removed (20e's breeding and killing
  runs, or a later probe).
- The mob shot's camera can end in a tree's canopy when the mob strolls under it (the open-air test runs at the search).
- Each cycle attempt scans its column from the world top (up to 289 x 12 scans when under the cap): no hitch measured
  yet; the cap usually stops the cycle first. A heightmap lookup if it shows in a profile.
- Hostiles (monster cycle every tick, the 0-7 light rule): 20c. Despawning of non-persistent mobs: 20c.
- Tags: `IsSheepFood` reads the gameplay tag each call (a map lookup); fine at sheep counts.
- From 20b2a: packs rolled once per column per session; pack members pile on chunk edges; the seen-set never shrinks.
