# Survival 20b1: pig, sheep, chicken, tempt and A* (record)

Lane `feature/survival-mobs` (base 8050f868, 20a merged with `feature/survival` f673fa9d), survival track T6, run 2.
20b was split (SM, 2026-10-15): this is 20b1; passive spawning is 20b2 ([the 20b2 handoff](2026-10-15-Mobs-Handoff-20b2.md)).
Switch: `foundation.Mobs` as in 20a (no new switch). `foundation.Mobs.Summon cow|pig|sheep|chicken`.

## 1. What was built

- **Kinds** (`FoundationMobs.cpp` `MobKinds`): the four farm animals are spawnable; tempt modifiers cow / pig / sheep /
  chicken 1.25 / 1.2 / 1.1 / 1.0 beside the panic ones 2 / 1.25 / 1.25 / 1.4 [survival.r10.ai_defaults, Codex 02 B].
- **Tempt** (`Think`): the nearest live player within 10 blocks (feet to feet) with one of the kind's breeding foods in
  either hand [F 5342-5347, hardcoded: cow and sheep wheat; pig carrot, potato, beetroot; chicken the six seeds]; it
  walks there at the tempt modifier, stops inside 2.5 blocks and looks at the player. Panic overrides it.
- **A*** (`FoundationMobPath.h/.cpp`, pure): stand cells (a partial block in the cell, or a 0.6-1 high floor below;
  fences and walls not standable; fluids avoided; headroom for the mob's height), 8 neighbours, diagonals only flat and
  without corner cutting, up one block (a rise of at most 1.25 with jump headroom), down at most 3, 256 expanded nodes,
  a partial path to the closest node otherwise. `FFoundationAuthority::GetPathCell` describes a cell from the same
  collision the mobs move against (boxes that start in the cell; a fence's top reaches 1.5). Stroll, panic and tempt
  plan a path when they start (`PlanPath`; a random target first settles on the stand cell of its column nearest the
  drawn height, so the vertical draw is now used); at most 4 searches per step, the rest steer straight until planned.
  `Steer` follows the waypoints (cell centres, reached within 0.5 block), then the target; a partial path ends at its
  last waypoint. The 40-step give-up stays as the fallback.
- **Chicken**: a falling chicken's vy x0.6 before its move [survival.r10.idle_and_sun]; the wing flap (speed -0.3 / +1.2
  per step, clamped; strength at least 1 in the air, x0.9; phase + 2 x strength) drawn as the wings' roll
  (sin(flap) + 1) x speed; the biped legs; an egg every 6000-11999 steps (uniform; S 5451-5457) as an `egg` item entity
  with `entity.chicken.egg`.
- **Sheep**: grazing (`Random.RandHelper(1000) == 0` standing in short grass or on a grass block; 40 steps; at 4 left the
  short grass breaks, else the grass block under it turns to dirt, through `World.Set`, behind `foundation.Mobs`); the
  head drops 9 px and nods (pi/5 + 0.21991149 sin(((t - 4)/32) x 28.7)); the wool layer (`sheep#wool`,
  `T_Mob_SheepWool`, white) drawn over the main layer.
- **DA_Mobs**: `MobImportData.LAYERS` adds layers after the eight main models; `UFoundationMobAssets::FindLayers`; the
  view draws every layer of a type (`FLayerDraw`). Reimported (`MOB_IMPORT PASS: ... 9 models (408 faces), 6 loot
  tables, 2 left out`); the re-saved but unchanged 20a textures and `M_Mob` were restored, so only `DA_Mobs` and the new
  `T_Mob_SheepWool` change.
- **Probe** (`-Suite mobs`): the overlay close-up (unhurt, hurt), pig + sheep + chicken, the chicken falling, the sheep
  grazing.

## 2. Decisions

- Split 20b into 20b1 / 20b2 (SM, Q1). Sheep grazing edits the world, behind the mobs switch, in memory like every edit
  (SM, Q2). Hardcoded breeding foods until the item tags exist (SM). A sound missing from `DA_Sounds` is a silent hook.
- Always plan with A* on a new goal (vanilla navigates every goal) rather than only when the straight line is blocked.
- Mobs take no fall damage yet, so the chicken's immunity holds trivially.
