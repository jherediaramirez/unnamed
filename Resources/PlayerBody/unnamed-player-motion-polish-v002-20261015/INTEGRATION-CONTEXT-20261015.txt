  one USkeletalMeshComponent; UFoundationBodyAnimInstance; no collision;
  absolute rendered transforms; pawn tick prerequisite. Missing clips reject
  the body. Initially hidden and OnlyTickPoseWhenRendered.
  UpdateBody, 196-228: velocity from simulation displacement; body/view yaw,

5. COVERAGE / KNOWN LIMITS TO CARRY FORWARD

The v001 manifest has 66 clips: idle 2, locomotion 24, stance transitions 2,
air/land 5, stationary turns 8, stand/crouch look poses 18, pickaxe hold/strikes
4, placeholders 3. The placeholders are UP_Low_Pose, UP_Flight_Placeholder and
UP_Death_Placeholder. Swimming strokes and a complete general use/equipment
library were not authored in UP. Inspect current gameplay for the real gap list.

Focused strike results from saved validation (isolated standing strikes):
  18 frames, frame 9 contact, shared frame 0=18 link; lower body retained.
  A/B/C tip speeds into contact about 24.10 / 21.06 / 15.19 m/s.
  Quarter-frame (240 Hz) samples report no pick/head, pick/arm or pick/torso
  penetration. Shoulder-joint overlap still reaches about 3.6 cm. These are
  sampled geometric checks, not proof of continuous or combined-pose clearance.

Unclosed findings from the motion review:
  - Look-grid corners can put the head roughly 9.5 cm into the shoulders.
    Hiding the local head does not resolve third-person deformation.
  - R-to-BR crosses a forward/backward gait-style boundary; inspect direction
    blends, not only individual planted clips.
  - World-space strike contact drops when the upper-body pose is placed over
    a lower locomotion/crouch pelvis. Recorded contact heights for a nominal
    1.25 m target were about 1.04-1.09 m walk, .98-1.02 sprint, .79-.85 crouch.
    Reproduce this composition before selecting an aim/contact correction.
  - Walk is a bent-knee jog at the fixed speed/cadence on these short legs.
  - Large proposed first-person body offsets are unapproved and can conflict
    with world foot planting, shadows, view switches and tool contact.
  - Original air-reel NLA hold/camera framing can obscure actual fall/land
    behavior; inspect actions directly. Preview defects are not runtime proof.
  - Use the corrected 60 fps arm MP4 for timing review; earlier comparison GIF
    timing was not an exact real-time representation.

The Blender round-trip report applies to v001 exports. No UP Unreal import,
compressed-animation acceptance, gameplay integration, networking or first-
person comfort acceptance was performed in this animation-polish work. Check
current engine assets afresh before making a broader claim.
